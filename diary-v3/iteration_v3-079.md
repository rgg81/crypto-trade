## 1. What was done

iter-v3/079 is the NINTH EXPLORATION of v3 cycle 2 (post-cycle-1-CONFIRMATION at iter-v3/070). Per `feedback_v3_strict_10_to_1_cadence.md`, cycle 2 runs 10 SEPARATE EXPLORATIONs (/071-/080) followed by 1 SEPARATE CONFIRMATION (/081 or later) — the 10th EXPLORATION is NOT collapsed into the CONFIRMATION.

The axis is a **single-axis NEW risk primitive — primitive 13, a conviction-weighted per-trade sizing primitive.** The implementation adds a `conviction_derate(confidence)` helper to `lgbm.py` and wires it into `LightGbmStrategy.get_signal`, replacing the hardcoded `weight = 100`. The mechanism:

- The M1 LightGBM model already computes a per-trade directional `confidence` inside `get_signal` (`confidence = max(proba_long, proba_short)` from the inner-ensemble mean `predict_proba`). Before /079 that value was computed and **discarded** — every signal got a flat `weight = 100`.
- `conviction_derate(confidence)` maps that confidence to an integer weight: `round(100 × clip((confidence − C_FLOOR) / (C_REF − C_FLOOR), W_MIN_FRAC, 1.0))` with `C_FLOOR = 0.50`, `C_REF = 0.65`, `W_MIN_FRAC = 0.50`. A confidence at/above `C_REF = 0.65` returns weight 100 (full size); a confidence at/below `C_FLOOR = 0.50` returns weight 50 (the `W_MIN_FRAC` floor); confidences in `[0.50, 0.65)` interpolate linearly.
- The hypothesis (brief Section 1): the M1 model's per-trade confidence carries per-trade edge that flat sizing wastes — de-rating low-conviction trades should lift the aggregate by under-weighting the trades most likely to lose.

Nothing else is touched — no feature added, no labeling change, no selection-gate change, no Optuna change. The secondary edit reverts /078's closed universe-revision axis: `V3_MODELS` goes from BCH/ADA/TRX back to **BCH/LDO/TRX** (the /060 anchor universe; the Critic confirmed this is a baseline-restore, not a second axis). `REQUIRED_GAP = 66 = (21+1)×3` is unchanged (the universe count stays 3).

## 4. The behavioral saturation — the axis barely engaged (7.5% vs predicted ≥25%)

**This is the central finding of the iteration.** The brief Section 4 predicted a behavioral-effect of **≥ 25%** of IS trades de-rated. The conviction-derate engaged only **7.5%** of IS trades (12/159) — a **3× behavioral-effect-predictor miss**, well below even the < 15% saturation falsifier.

**The root cause is a constant-placement defect — C_REF=0.65 was placed at the threshold-saturation point of the confidence distribution, not its busy region:**

- The de-rate window is `[C_FLOOR = 0.50, C_REF = 0.65)`. A trade is de-rated only if its M1 confidence falls inside that window.
- The `/067 _inference_threshold_floor = 0.60` is a hard floor: every signal that survives to become a trade has `confidence ≥ 0.60` by construction. So the *effective* de-rate window is not `[0.50, 0.65)` — it is the much narrower `[0.60, 0.65)` band.
- The walk-forward Optuna search, at n_trials=35, tunes a per-`(symbol, month)` confidence/inference threshold. The engineering report establishes that those tuned thresholds concentrate **at or above 0.65** — at or above C_REF. With the per-cell threshold sitting at/above C_REF, the surviving signals' confidences pile up at/above 0.65, collapsing the effective de-rate window `[0.60, 0.65)` toward empty.
- The net effect: only the small residual tail of trades whose confidence happened to land in `[0.60, 0.65)` got de-rated — 12 of 159, 7.5%.

C_REF = 0.65 was an **a-priori guess**, and it landed at the threshold-saturation point of the confidence distribution rather than in its populated region. It was not informed by the empirical per-trade confidence distribution — because **that distribution is not persisted in any report artifact** (per-trade `confidence` is computed inside `get_signal` and discarded). The brief had no way to place C_REF empirically and guessed; the guess sat on top of the Optuna threshold pile-up.

This is the same class of finding as the cycle-2 `feedback_v3_axis_saturation_predictor.md` discipline: a behavioral-effect predictor for an axis gated on a model-internal threshold must explicitly model the interaction with the Optuna-tuned threshold distribution. The /079 predictor did not — it modelled the confidence distribution as if the de-rate window were `[0.50, 0.65)` and free of the `_inference_threshold_floor=0.60` clamp and the Optuna pile-up.

## 5. The under-tested-not-dead adjudication — the axis is PARKED, not closed (Critic Adjudication #3)

**The conviction-derate axis is UNDER-TESTED at these a-priori constants — it is NOT a genuinely dead signal.** This is the Critic's Adjudication #3 verdict, and it is the reason the axis is **PARKED, not closed.**

The directional hypothesis has **weak but real support.** The engineering report isolated the 12 de-rated (low-conviction) IS trades against the 147 non-de-rated:

| Group | n IS trades | mean net_pnl_pct | win rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| De-rated (low-conviction) | 12 | **−0.019%** | **33.3%** |
| Non-de-rated | 147 | **+0.476%** | **38.0%** |

The 12 de-rated trades **did underperform** — lower mean PnL and lower win rate than the non-de-rated group. The hypothesis "M1 confidence carries per-trade edge that flat sizing wastes" was **directionally confirmed** on the available sample. It simply did not get enough trade population (7.5% incidence) to move the aggregate Sharpe (+0.0052 IS). **A genuinely dead axis would show de-rated trades performing AT or ABOVE the non-de-rated group** — the opposite of what was observed. Low conviction did mean lower realized edge; the primitive just had almost nothing to act on.

**The structural caveat.** There is a real possibility the engageable population is intrinsically modest. If Optuna's IS-optimized confidence thresholds intrinsically sit ≥ 0.65 for a hard 8h directional-classification problem, then the model's surviving signals **are nearly all high-conviction by construction** — and a conviction-derate has structurally little to act on regardless of constant choice. A re-attempt at a higher C_REF (0.70-0.75) would widen the window into the populated band, but the gain is bounded by whatever per-trade conviction edge exists *above* 0.65 — which this EDA could not measure, because per-trade confidence is not persisted.

**Consequence: the axis is PARKED.** A re-attempt is permissible ONLY with (a) re-designed constants AND (b) the per-trade M1 `confidence` distribution persisted across the walk-forward FIRST — so a re-attempt's C_REF is placed **empirically, in the populated region**, not by another a-priori guess. The instrumentation gap (per-trade confidence not in `trades.csv`) is what blocked /079 from a fair test; closing that gap is the precondition for any re-attempt. The conviction-derate axis is not on the closed list with the Kaufman path-efficiency feature, the regime-conditional kill switch (primitive 9), and the LDO→ADA universe swap — it is parked pending the instrumentation.

## 6. Look-ahead audit + pure-weight-scalar verification

**Look-ahead — clean by full 5-step chain audit (Critic Check 1 / Adjudication #2).** The Critic traced the conviction-derate's data dependency end-to-end:

1. `conviction_derate` (`lgbm.py:114-137`) is a pure arithmetic function — `round(100 × clip((confidence − c_floor)/(c_ref − c_floor), w_min_frac, 1.0))`. No closures, no global state, no I/O. The test file `test_conviction_derate.py::TestLookAheadClean` independently verifies `__closure__ is None` and statically scans the source for forbidden references (`self.`, `pd.`, `open_time`, `proba`, `models`) — all absent.
2. `confidence` is computed (`lgbm.py:663-676`) from `proba = mean([m.predict_proba(feat_df)[0] for m in self._models])` on `feat_row = self._month_features.get((symbol, open_time))`.
3. `_month_features` (`lgbm.py:550-559`) is loaded via `load_features_range(..., split.test_start_ms, split.test_end_ms, ...)` — the test-month bar-close OHLCV-derived features for the bar being decided, knowable at bar `t`'s close (the decision point). Standard bar-close pattern.
4. `self._models` are trained inside `_train_for_month` strictly on the embargoed past walk-forward window (`train_indices = where((open_time ≥ train_start_ms) & (open_time < train_end_ms))`, with `train_end_ms` carrying the embargo). The production model never sees a future bar.
5. The conviction-derate (`lgbm.py:754`) reads `confidence` only AFTER it is already in scope from step 2 — it introduces **zero new data dependency**. It is a pure arithmetic transform of a value that was already look-ahead-clean.

**Pure-weight-scalar — STRUCTURALLY GUARANTEED, not merely empirical (Critic Adjudication #2).** The Critic confirmed the property is structurally necessary:

- The conviction-derate (`lgbm.py:754`) is the LAST statement before `return Signal(...)` and modifies ONLY the `weight` field. Every trade-suppressing return path (`NO_SIGNAL` at lines 654/660/686/706/719) executes BEFORE line 754, unchanged.
- `conviction_derate` returns an int in `[50, 100]` — `test_conviction_derate.py::test_weight_never_zero` verifies across a 10001-point grid it never returns 0 (0 is the only `Signal.weight` that could suppress a trade downstream).
- Therefore the set of bars emitting a `Signal` vs `NO_SIGNAL` is identical with or without the de-rate — the `(symbol, open_time)` roster key set **cannot change.** The QE reports, and the Critic verified, that the IS+OOS key roster is bit-identical to /077's /060-config roster: **0 added, 0 removed**, all six symbol-split pairs. IS mean-duration delta = **0.0000 candles** (a weight scalar touches no barrier).

**The OOS +0.0713 is largely a data-extent artifact.** Because the roster is bit-identical, the +0.0713 OOS Sharpe lift is not the conviction-derate producing edge. The OOS mean-duration delta is +0.0194 candles — the last LDO `end_of_data` trade had its `close_time` extend ~2 candles (~16h) between the /077 and /079 runs (days apart; the OOS data extent grew). That data-extent drift on one trade plus the thin 7.5% de-rate engagement is the entirety of the +0.0713. The conviction-derate's own contribution to the OOS Sharpe is negligible.
