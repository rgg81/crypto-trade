Cointegration (Engle-Granger) tested on the formation window only; tradeability and spread stationarity measured on the SUBSEQUENT out-of-formation window — the way a walk-forward pairs book would actually select and trade.

**The verdict is NO-GO on three independent, decisive gates** (full evidence: `analysis/iteration_v3-095/NO_GO_NOTE.md` + the T1-T4 CSVs):

1. **Out-of-formation cointegration SURVIVAL — the binding failure.** For every pair selected as cointegrated in the 24-month formation window, the spread was ADF-tested on the *next* month using the formation hedge ratio. Median out-of-formation ADF p-value **0.4524**; only **14.77%** of in-formation-cointegrated spreads are still cointegrated (ADF p<0.10) the very next month. The formation-window cointegration does NOT survive into the window where it would be traded — the prep-memo Candidate-B caveat, quantified. ~85% of the time a cointegration-selected pairs book trades a spread that is no longer mean-reverting.
2. **The faithful walk-forward BOOK does not clear cost.** The stress test ran the actual strategy a v3 cointegration runner would deploy — per-window top-10 pairs by formation Engle-Granger p-value (filtered to a tradeable 2-60-bar half-life), z-score spread signal traded market-neutral on the subsequent month, 0.20%/round-trip cost — and aggregated one monthly book PnL series. NET monthly Sharpe **-0.0770** (strict z-entry 2.0) / **-0.0236** (loose z-entry 1.5); the book is net-negative AND **gross-negative** in both variants. v3's /059 baseline is OOS monthly Sharpe +0.58 and the merge floor is +1.0 — a new strategy class that cannot clear an IS NET monthly Sharpe of even +0.30, generously parameterized and before any OOS haircut, has no realistic path to merge and a NEGATIVE OOS is foreseeable.
3. **Trade-rate floor failed by ~3×.** The faithful book fires **2.5-3.7 trades/month** vs the ≥10/month project hard gate (`feedback_trade_rate_floor.md`). Median spread half-life ~60 bars (20 days) gives only ~1.5 mean-reversion cycles inside a 90-bar monthly window, so 2/3 of selected pair-windows never complete a round-trip.

A first-pass EDA returned a fragile GO (mean "persistence" 0.70) — but the stress test exposed this as an **in-sample-overlap artifact**: persistence was measured on the Engle-Granger p-value across adjacent 24-month formation windows that share 23/24 months (~96% identical data), so the statistic is mechanically autocorrelated and is NOT evidence the spread is tradeable next month. The honest, non-overlapping metric — out-of-formation spread ADF — returns 14.77% survival, not 70%. Recorded methodology lesson: cointegration "persistence" measured on overlapping formation windows is an in-sample-overlap artifact; the load-bearing metric is out-of-formation spread stationarity.
