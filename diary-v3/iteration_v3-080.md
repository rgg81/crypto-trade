## 4. The fix-and-rerun — the empty-`confidence`-column defect, fix `d71adf6`, and the clean-and-legitimate certification

**The first /080 run produced a bit-identical-roster NULL-RESULT — the correct classification — but the `confidence` column in `trades.csv` was EMPTY.** This is the load-bearing process event of the iteration, and it is worth recording precisely.

**The defect.** /080's deliverable is the persisted per-trade `confidence`. The first implementation (`8e7e62b`) added the `confidence` field to `Signal`/`Order`/`TradeResult`, forwarded it through `make_order`/`make_result`, and wrote the `confidence` column in `_write_trades_csv` — but it missed the two **risk-wrapper `Signal`-reconstruction sites**. `RiskV2Wrapper.get_signal` (`risk_v2.py`) and `RiskV3Wrapper.get_signal` (`risk_v3.py`) each *reconstruct* a fresh `Signal` (to apply their position-size scalars) rather than passing the M1 `Signal` through. Both reconstruction sites built the new `Signal` *without* forwarding `confidence` — so by the time the `Signal` reached the backtest's `make_order`, `confidence` had been silently dropped to `None`. Every row of `trades.csv` carried an empty `confidence`. The deliverable — the whole point of the iteration — was not actually delivered by the first run.

**The catch.** The QE's own engineering report caught the empty column. This is the engineering-report verification discipline working as designed: the QE did not rubber-stamp the run; it inspected the deliverable artifact, found it degenerate, and reported it. The orchestrator authorized a fix-and-rerun.

**The fix — commit `d71adf6`.** The fix adds `confidence=sig.confidence` at the exactly-two wrapper `Signal`-reconstruction sites — `risk_v2.py` (the `RiskV2Wrapper` reconstruction) and `risk_v3.py` (the `RiskV3Wrapper` primitive-12 regime-size-scalar reconstruction branch). It also adds an extended wrapper-chain regression test (`tests/strategies/ml/test_confidence_passthrough.py`): `TestWrapperChainConfidencePassthrough` routes a `Signal` with a known `confidence` through both wrappers with gates disabled and asserts the field survives — including a sub-test that injects a `_btc_trend_lookup` to FORCE the primitive-12 reconstruction branch (the second drop point) and asserts both the scalar applied AND `confidence` preserved; and `TestNoDecisionPathReadsConfidence` adds a static scan over `src/crypto_trade/*.py` asserting no `if/while .confidence` decision branch exists outside an allow-list — so the defect cannot recur and `confidence` cannot silently become a decision input.

**The Critic's certification — clean and legitimate (review Adjudication #2).** The Critic certified the fix-and-rerun methodologically clean on three grounds:
- **(a) The fix is pure passive metadata.** The Critic audited both wrapper reconstruction sites. At each, the reconstructed `Signal`'s `weight` is computed from `sig.weight × scale` arithmetic that does NOT consult `confidence`; `confidence` is not in any gate (`_zscore_ood`, `_hurst_ood`, `_adx_gate_fails`, `_low_vol_filter_fails`, `_per_symbol_cap_scale`, `_vol_scale`, the drawdown brake) and not in any conditional. The fix forwards an already-look-ahead-clean value and touches no decision path.
- **(b) The classification is invariant.** The re-run `comparison.csv` is **bit-identical to the pre-fix run** (IS +0.8236, OOS +0.2908, 159/103 trades). Forwarding a passive metadata field changed no trade, no barrier, no weight — exactly as a pure-metadata fix must. The bit-identical re-run roster is itself the proof that the fix touched no decision path.
- **(c) It is a legitimate Phase-6 correction, not a post-BLOCK rerun.** The defect was a *deliverable wiring* defect — the iteration's instrument was mis-wired — caught by the QE's own engineering report *before* the Critic review. It is a pre-Critic Phase-6 correction of a deliverable defect, which is legitimate; it is not a re-run in response to a Critic BLOCK (which would be a different and more scrutinized situation). The orchestrator's authorization of the fix-and-rerun is correct process.

The fix-and-rerun is the methodology working: a deliverable defect was caught at the engineering-report stage by the QE, corrected with a pure-metadata fix plus a recurrence-preventing regression test, and the correction left the classification bit-identically invariant.
