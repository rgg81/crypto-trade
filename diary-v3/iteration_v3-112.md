## 8. Lessons

1. **The pooled-full model architecture is markedly worse than per-symbol — pooling 3 heterogeneous symbols into one model destroys the strong symbol.** This is the structural finding of iter-v3/112. v3 has trained one model per symbol for all 111 prior iterations; the pooled-full architecture (ONE LightGBM on the concatenated BCH+LDO+TRX panel) produced IS −1.1236 / OOS −0.7228 — decisively below both merge bars and far below the per-symbol /059 baseline (IS +1.0894 / OOS +0.5791). The mechanism is heterogeneity-driven dilution: BCH, LDO, and TRX have different feature→label structure (BCH is the only one with a real per-symbol edge — 95.76% of /059's IS PnL, 49.4% IS win rate), and concatenating their panels into one training frame forces the model to fit a blurred cross-symbol rule that fits none of them. BCH — the strong symbol — was inverted by the dilution from a strong OOS positive to a −42.80 OOS loss at a 20.7% win rate. Generalizable rule: **a pooled cross-symbol model only helps when the pooled symbols share feature→label structure; pooling heterogeneous symbols dilutes the strong symbol and is net-destructive.** The v3 universe (BCH/LDO/TRX) is heterogeneous by construction — it is the survivor set of v3's symbol search, three symbols kept for *individually* surviving, not for sharing structure — so full pooling was always going to dilute. The pooled-full architecture is **CLOSED for cycle 6.**

2. **An aggregate-NO-GO EDA with only a per-symbol-conditional GO is a weak GO — and the production backtest confirmed it.** The /112 EDA's aggregate held-out-AUC horse race returned a clean NO-GO (T5: pooled AUC lift −0.0008, positive in 1 of 3 IS thirds). The only GO signal was per-symbol-conditional — the LDO rescue (T11/T12). The brief proceeded under the "sharpened-GO" PRIME DIRECTIVE, which is correct procedure, but the realized OOS Δ (−0.8631) landed below the brief's own 80% predicted-interval lower bound. This replicates the /111 lesson (a thin EDA proxy inverts through the production Optuna + 7-gate pipeline) in a new form: a *per-symbol* GO inside an *aggregate* wash also does not transfer net-positive. Generalizable rule: when an EDA returns an aggregate NO-GO and only a per-symbol-conditional GO, the brief's predicted-effect interval must be weighted heavily toward the aggregate NO-GO, and the central estimate should be at-or-below zero — a per-symbol rescue is not evidence of a net-positive aggregate effect.

3. **The QR's pre-registered failure mode FIRED — pre-registration discipline works.** The brief Section 7 named mode 1 (pooling dilutes the sample-rich symbols → BCH collapse) as the single most plausible OOS failure, and explicitly flagged BCH — the 95.76%-of-IS-PnL symbol — as the symbol the architecture most needed not to harm, citing the BASELINE_V3.md cycle-1 fragility note. The as-run result is exactly that: BCH inverted to −42.80. This is a clean prediction-calibration win and is recorded as such — when the EDA exposes a credible downside mechanism (here: the T3 BCH/TRX dilution measured at −0.007/−0.021 AUC), pre-registering it as the named failure mode and then confirming it in the backtest is the iteration working as designed. The honest qualifier (Lesson 2): the magnitude was under-predicted — the brief framed the BCH dilution as "light", the realized collapse was severe.

4. **For partial-pooling or any future cross-symbol-architecture axis, pre-commit a runnable per-symbol EXPLORATION-mode reference.** The /112 brief's falsifier #2 and Section 8 C4 (LDO OOS `net_pnl_pct` improves vs the per-symbol EXPLORATION-mode LDO) could not be evaluated against a *measured* baseline — the per-symbol EXPLORATION-mode LDO was never run, so the LDO rescue magnitude is only readable against the /059 CONFIRMATION pattern (Critic Recommendation 1). Generalizable rule for the iter-v3/113 QR: if a partial-pooling axis is pursued, the brief must pre-commit a runnable per-symbol EXPLORATION-mode LDO reference (a single per-symbol LDO cell at `--exploration` settings) so the rescue delta is a measured quantity, not an inferred one.
