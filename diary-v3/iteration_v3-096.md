iter-v3/096's axis — chosen by the QR with a committed EDA-driven quantitative basis per `feedback_v3_axis_selection_quant_discipline.md` — is the one architecture v3 has never tried: a **POOLED cross-symbol model**, one LightGBM trained on the concatenated BCH+LDO+TRX sample (v1's Model A construction: BTC+ETH pooled, proven to work in v1), replacing the per-symbol-model architecture every v3 iteration in cycles 1-4 has used. Pooling triples the training rows per model — the textbook fix for the IS-overfitting root cause the /088 closeout named.

The axis was chosen over the /095-closeout-recommended non-tree sequence model — and over the large-universe restructure and regime-switching TSMOM — **precisely because it admits a cheap Phase-1 GO/NO-GO**. The sequence model is a model-architecture question that can only be confirmed by a full build + backtest (the fail-fast directive disfavors an un-gateable multi-hour bet sight-unseen; and iter-v3/016's LightGBM→XGBoost model-arch change was the worst OOS Δ in v3 history at −2.53). The large-universe restructure was *already falsified* — iter-v3/088's T5 universe-width sweep tested widths 3/5/8/12/22 and found rank-IC negative at every width with the IC information ratio worsening monotonically (−0.078 at k=3 → −0.129 at k=22); the 2025 literature confirms crypto breadth deteriorated (median token −79%) — going 22→100 extrapolates a curve already pointing down. Regime-switching TSMOM is the prep-memo's lowest-ambition candidate. The pooled model, by contrast, has a decisive premise — *the feature→label map transfers across symbols* — that is cheaply EDA-testable: pooling adds signal only if a model trained on {A,B} generalizes to held-out symbol {C}.

Phase 1 was an explicit hard FAIL-FAST GO/NO-GO. Two committed IS-only EDA scripts (`analysis/iteration_v3-096/`, walk-forward-faithful per `feedback_v3_eda_walkforward_faithful` — 24-month listing burn-in dropped, strictly `open_time < OOS_CUTOFF_MS = 1742774400000`) measured the leave-one-symbol-out (LOSO) cross-symbol transfer rank-IC: train a LightGBM on the other two symbols' IS bars, measure rank-IC of its prediction vs the realized /059-production triple-barrier directional label on the held-out symbol's IS bars — rows AND a symbol the model never saw. The triple-barrier label was computed with the exact /059 params (ATR 2.0/1.0, timeout 21 candles, fee 0.1%) replicated from `labeling.py:label_trades`; the within-symbol benchmark used a purged 5-fold CV with a 22-candle embargo.

**The verdict is NO-GO** (full evidence: `analysis/iteration_v3-096/NO_GO_NOTE.md` + the T1-T7 CSVs). The T5 headline transfer IC was **+0.0698** — 90% of the within-symbol benchmark (+0.0775) — and cleared the pre-registered gate-1 floor (+0.030), so the headline *looked* promising. But fail-fast targets KNOWN failures, not genuinely-uncertain tests, so the borderline headline was adjudicated honestly with a second script before any verdict: a block-bootstrap 95% CI (2000 resamples of contiguous 21-bar blocks = the label horizon, preserving the serial dependence of overlapping triple-barrier labels). The adjudication is decisive: **every one of the three held-out symbols' transfer ICs has a 95% CI that straddles zero** — BCH [−0.0159, +0.1502], LDO [−0.0588, +0.3478], TRX [−0.0921, +0.0767]. The +0.0698 headline is a point estimate with no statistical support; the cross-symbol transfer signal is not significant on any symbol. TRX's transfer IC point estimate is negative (−0.0065, retention −22%) — pooling can *hurt*, not just fail to help (the iter-v3/094 sign-inconsistency failure mode at the model level), which tripped the pre-registered sign-consistency gate. Only **4 of 14** features have a univariate IC sign that is consistent across all 3 symbols — a pooled model would be fed contradictory training signal on the majority of its features. The pooled-model axis's decisive premise is not supported; a 1.6h pooled-model backtest would be a coin-flip-grade bet, not a high-information test.

The honest scope of the kill, recorded: this does NOT prove pooling is universally dead. The within-symbol benchmark itself is thin on BCH (+0.025) and TRX (+0.029) — LDO (+0.178) is the only symbol with material per-symbol signal. The 14-feature price stack carries thin, non-transferable signal on BCH/TRX *period*; pooling cannot manufacture cross-symbol signal that is not there. The binding constraint is the **signal/label**, not the per-symbol-vs-pooled architecture — consistent with the cycle 1-3 finding that the per-symbol price problem is tapped out.

This is exactly the foreseeable-modest outcome `feedback_fail_fast.md` says resources should NOT go to — and it was killed for the cost of **two committed IS-only EDA scripts: no `fetch`, no runner, no backtest, no Critic, no agent dispatch.** `OOS_CUTOFF_DATE = 2025-03-24` and `training_months = 24` are untouched; every measurement is strictly IS-only; OOS was never touched.

**Recommended iter-v3/097 axis — a label-construction re-architecture: a meta-labeled or volatility-scaled target, EDA-gated on whether a re-engineered label lifts the within-symbol CV IC on BCH and TRX above the +0.025/+0.029 triple-barrier baseline this EDA measured.** Two facts from this EDA point the way: (1) LDO is the one symbol with real signal (within IC +0.178, 7× BCH/TRX) — the thin-signal problem is concentrated in BCH/TRX; (2) the 14-feature stack, not the model, is the binding constraint. The cleanest cheaply-gateable form keeps the per-symbol architecture and the 14-feature stack fixed and tests whether a *different label* — a meta-label (López de Prado AFML Ch. 3: a primary rule sets the side, a secondary classifier predicts act/no-act) or a volatility-normalized return label — produces a materially higher within-symbol CV IC on BCH+TRX. That is a Phase-1-gateable question (recompute within-symbol IC under the new label; GO only if BCH and TRX both lift materially), attacks the actual binding constraint, and is a genuine re-architecture. NOTE: iter-v3/017 meta-labeling closed only the over-filter failure mode at one configuration — a within-symbol-IC-lift EDA gate is a different, cheaper test. Fallback: prep-memo Candidate C (regime-switching TSMOM), lowest-ambition.
