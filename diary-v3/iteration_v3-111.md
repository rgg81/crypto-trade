## 8. Lessons

1. **The thin EDA feature-AUC screen margin did not survive the production Optuna + 7-gate stack — symbol-selection-by-feature-AUC-screen is a closed method for cycle 6.** The /110 EDA's screen ranked CRV/AAVE/GRT/ADA above the incumbents on broad-population feature→label AUC, and the IS gated 2:1-barrier book proxy for the universe was +0.25 monthly Sharpe (`T9`). The brief (Section 2.7) disclosed honestly that the margin was thin — only CRV cleared the strict 3-criterion GO bar; AAVE/GRT/ADA were marginal (clearing on AUC-margin-over-q50 and permutation p, but not on the q95 margin). Under the corrected `triple_barrier` runner, the production pipeline — 35-trial Optuna search + the 7-gate RiskV2 stack — did not convert the +0.25 IS gated proxy; it **inverted it to −0.31**. Generalizable rule: a thin broad-population feature-AUC screen margin is not a reliable predictor of the production gated book. The screen's gated-tail proxy (`T9`) does not apply the production Optuna search or the 7-gate stack, and a +0.25 proxy edge built on a sub-q95 AUC margin can invert sign through that machinery. The symbol-selection-by-feature-AUC-screen method is **closed for cycle 6** — not "symbol selection" as an axis (universe choice remains a legitimate structural lever), but specifically the *select-by-feature→label-AUC-screen* construction.
