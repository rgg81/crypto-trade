## 3. What Worked / What Failed

### What Worked

1. **The multi-frequency daily-feature axis is implemented look-ahead-free — Check 1 PASS, verified at source.** This is the structural deliverable of iter-v3/113 and the most important "worked" item. The single largest risk of a multi-frequency feature is a leak through the cross-frequency alignment, and the Critic traced it line-by-line in `src/crypto_trade/features_v3/multifreq_v3.py`. The daily-bar `day_close` is the last 8h candle's `close_time` (= `23:59:59.999` of the UTC day). The `merge_asof(direction="backward", left_on="open_time", right_on="day_close")` matches every 8h decision candle in day D — opening at 00/08/16 — to daily bar **D−1**, because day D's own `day_close` (`23:59:59.999 of D`) exceeds all three intra-day `open_time`s and is not selected. Every 8h candle sees only strictly-past daily data. All 8 daily features are past-only on the daily grid (no centered windows, no future shift). The adversarial `test_past_only_property` genuinely tests the property (appends 90 future rows, asserts row-60 daily features bit-identical to `<1e-10`); `test_day_close_alignment` independently asserts `day_close ≤ open_time` on every matched row. No look-ahead found — the multi-frequency aggregation, a genuinely new look-ahead surface, is causally clean.

2. **The 22-feature multi-frequency stack genuinely ran — clean, no scope creep — Check 8 PASS, verified at source.** `run.log` line 3 enumerates all 8 daily features by name in the PASS line — they were not silently dropped; line 64 confirms `22 feature columns`; the `multifreq_v3` module is registered in `GROUP_REGISTRY` and appended to `V3_FEATURE_COLUMNS_TOP_N` in brief-mandated order. The per-symbol architecture was correctly restored from /112's pooled build (`run.log` shows three `MODEL v3-113-{BCH,LDO,TRX}` blocks, no `[POOLED]` string). Exactly one substantive knob changed (`V3_FEATURE_COLUMNS` 14 → 22); `label_mode=triple_barrier` preserved; track isolation holds (`multifreq_v3.py` imports only numpy/pandas). The NEGATIVE is a genuine, attributable property of the axis.

3. **The daily representation carries permutation-validated STANDALONE signal — the EDA's T5 finding stands.** The EDA's headline (T5) — a LightGBM on the 8 daily features alone scored held-out AUC 0.5275, clearing its no-signal permutation q95 at p=0.00 — is a genuine, non-spurious result, and it is recorded as a partial positive: the coarser bar frequency IS a different signal-to-noise regime, exactly as the /109 diary §3 anticipated. The daily representation, in isolation, sees structure the 8h representation cannot. This is real and is not retracted by the NEGATIVE verdict.

4. **The Critic review was a single round.** Every verdict-driving check is unambiguous; Round 2 was skipped because no QR clarification can change the OVERALL — the iteration is EXPLORATION-NEGATIVE on the IS-Sharpe axis (C1, Δ −0.6704) regardless of how the contested Check 4 resolves.

### What Failed

1. **The standalone daily signal washed in the combined stack — the EDA's own T4 caveat materialized.** The decisive failure. The EDA's T5 proved the daily family carries standalone signal (AUC 0.5275), but T4 had already shown the **combined** 8h+daily-8 stack does NOT clear its own permutation null (pooled AUC 0.5015, p=0.39). The Phase-6 backtest confirmed that caveat: the small daily edge (~2.75 AUC-points over chance), concatenated into a 22-feature model whose 14-feature 8h component is near-noise, did not survive into a robust trade-selection rule. The book landed flat — IS monthly Sharpe +0.1621, OOS +0.0015. A real-but-small standalone signal does not transfer net-positive through the production Optuna + 7-gate pipeline when it is diluted into a larger near-noise stack.

2. **IS monthly Sharpe collapsed −0.6704 vs the /060 anchor.** Worse than a wash — the IS book did not merely fail to improve, it collapsed from the /060 anchor's +0.8325 to +0.1621. Adding 8 features (4 of them IC-redundant with the 8h stack) gave production Optuna at `n_trials=35` more search space to overfit IS noise — the `feedback_v3_inert_features_at_higher_budget.md` mechanism: a larger feature space, when the new features add no independent signal, lets Optuna fit IS-suboptimal regions. The pre-registered Section 8 criterion C1 fails at Δ −0.6704; the NEGATIVE falsifier (IS Δ < −0.10) fires.

3. **The axis is behaviorally inert — the IS trade roster moved only 1.89%.** Below the brief's pre-registered 8% threshold (Section 4.3 / Section 7 Mode 3). The 8 daily features carried non-trivial split importance (rank 15-22 of 22, importance 194-306 portfolio-level) but barely shifted the confidence ranking near the gate threshold — the 22-feature gated roster is materially the 14-feature roster. The daily features were consulted by the trees but not by the gates that matter.

4. **The brief's predicted-effect interval was modestly too optimistic on the IS axis.** The brief's IS monthly Sharpe point estimate was +0.90 (80% interval [+0.35, +1.25]); the realized +0.1621 landed **below the interval's lower bound**. The OOS prediction was better-calibrated: the brief's OOS point estimate was +0.30 (80% interval [−0.25, +0.85]) and the realized +0.0015 is comfortably inside it — the wide, caveat-weighted OOS interval (per the iter-v3/112 Lesson 2 discipline) did its job. The miss is the IS axis: the brief predicted the daily features would "approximately preserve or modestly improve" IS contribution (Section 4.2), and instead IS collapsed. The honest lesson (Section 8 Lesson 3): when the EDA's combined-stack horse race fails its own permutation null, the IS interval — not only the OOS interval — should be centered at or below the anchor.

## 4. Phase 8 — the axis closes clean

The multi-frequency daily-feature axis is now cleanly tested and closed. Adding 8 coarser-frequency 1-day-bar features to the 14-feature 8h stack — the cleanest possible multi-frequency design (zero new data dependency, an exact look-ahead-free aggregation of the 8h candles, a trivially-auditable causal as-of-join) — produced IS monthly Sharpe +0.1621 / OOS monthly Sharpe +0.0015: an IS collapse of −0.6704 vs the /060 anchor and a flat OOS book. The mechanism of the failure is understood and attributable, and it was visible in the EDA before the backtest: the daily family carries a real but **small** standalone signal (T5, AUC 0.5275), but the **combined** 22-feature stack does not clear its own permutation null (T4, p=0.39) — the small daily edge washes when concatenated into a model dominated by the larger, near-noise 14-feature 8h stack, and the larger search space lets Optuna overfit IS noise. The behavioral-inertia falsifier confirms it from the production side: the gated roster moved only 1.89%, so the daily features — for all their non-trivial tree-split importance — did not change the high-confidence trade decisions the 7 gates act on.

Per the pre-registered Section 8 taxonomy, iter-v3/113 is catalogued **EXPLORATION-NEGATIVE — the multi-frequency daily-feature axis is CLOSED for cycle 6.** Two qualifications are recorded honestly:

- **The standalone daily signal (T5) is real and is not retracted.** The NEGATIVE verdict is about the *combined* stack, not the daily representation in isolation. T5 stands as a genuine finding — the coarser bar frequency is a different signal-to-noise regime. What the iteration closes is the specific design of *concatenating* coarser-frequency aggregations into the existing 22-feature 8h stack: that design washes. A future multi-frequency axis that *replaces* rather than *augments* the 8h stack, or that builds a separate coarser-frequency model, is a structurally different design the /113 result does not close — but it is not a cycle-6 priority (Section 9).
- **The IC carve-out the brief invoked (Section 4.4) is contested and does NOT extend to this axis.** Per Critic Recommendation 1, the `feedback_v3_engineered_feature_pivot.md` IC carve-out is scoped to algebraically-composed Category 2 features (`feature_A × sign(feature_B − threshold)`, sharing variance with primitives BY CONSTRUCTION). A coarser-frequency aggregation of the same OHLCV is **not** algebraically composed from an 8h feature — there is no formula `d_ret_5d = f(8h features)`; the 0.849 IC is empirical, not constructive. By the carve-out's own text a non-composed feature is Category 1, for which the strict |IC| < 0.70 gate applies in full. The 4 high-IC daily features (d_ret_5d 0.849, d_ret_10d 0.778, d_realvol_10 0.804, d_close_pos_20 0.852) are Category 1 redundancies. This does not change the /113 verdict (NEGATIVE on C1 regardless) but it is recorded as a process correction (Lesson 4 / Section 9).

## 8. Lessons

1. **The multi-frequency daily-feature axis (coarser 1-day bars augmenting the 8h stack) is CLOSED for cycle 6 — behaviorally inert and IS-collapsing.** This is the structural finding of iter-v3/113. Adding 8 coarser-frequency 1-day-bar features to the 14-feature 8h stack — the cleanest possible multi-frequency design, verified look-ahead-free at source — produced IS monthly Sharpe +0.1621 (Δ −0.6704 vs the /060 anchor) and a flat OOS book (+0.0015). The mechanism is understood and was visible in the EDA: the daily family carries a real but **small** standalone signal (T5, AUC 0.5275), but the **combined** 22-feature stack does not clear its own permutation null (T4, p=0.39) — the small daily edge washes when concatenated into a model dominated by the larger, near-noise 14-feature 8h stack, and the enlarged feature space lets Optuna overfit IS noise. Generalizable rule: **a coarser-frequency aggregation that carries standalone signal but does not survive concatenation into the combined stack's permutation null is an INERT augmentation — augmenting the existing stack with it degrades IS without lifting OOS.** The multi-frequency daily-feature axis joins /085's funding family and /086's basis family as a CLOSED feature-family axis; the 8 daily features are dropped (`V3_FEATURE_COLUMNS` reverts to the 14-feature stack for iter-v3/114). Per `feedback_v3_inert_features_at_higher_budget.md`, the axis must NOT be retested at a higher Optuna budget — a behaviorally-inert axis at n_trials=35 does not become live at a higher budget.

2. **A standalone-feature EDA test is not a sufficient GO gate — the combined-stack test is.** The /113 EDA ran both: T5 (the 8 daily features ALONE) cleared its permutation null decisively (AUC 0.5275, p=0.00), but T4 (the combined 8h+daily-8 stack) did NOT (AUC 0.5015, p=0.39). The production backtest tracked T4, not T5: the iteration is what the *combined* stack does, because the production model is the combined stack. Generalizable rule (and Critic Recommendation 3): **for any feature-addition axis, the EDA's decisive GO gate is whether the COMBINED stack (existing features + candidate) clears a permutation null — not whether the candidate clears it standalone.** A standalone signal that washes in concatenation is a pre-visible NEGATIVE; spending an EXPLORATION slot to confirm it is spending a scarce slot on a known outcome. Cycle-6 slots 5-10 should prefer axes whose EDA *combined-stack* test clears its permutation null.

3. **When the EDA's combined-stack horse race fails its permutation null, the modal classification is NEGATIVE — not INERT — and the predicted-effect interval (IS and OOS) must be centered at or below the anchor.** The /113 brief pre-registered the modal outcome as INERT and judged behavioral inertia low-probability; the reality was NEGATIVE and behavioral inertia fired. The miss has a clear root cause: a combined-stack permutation-null failure means the candidate features add no independent signal, and adding no-signal features to the stack gives Optuna more room to overfit IS noise — so the modal outcome is an IS *degradation*, not a benign wash. The brief defended only the OOS interval (centered +0.30, lower bound −0.25 — the realized +0.0015 landed inside it); the IS interval ([+0.35, +1.25]) was too optimistic and the realized +0.1621 fell below it. Generalizable rule: when the EDA's combined-stack test fails its permutation null, pre-register the modal classification as NEGATIVE and center BOTH the IS and OOS predicted-effect intervals at or below the anchor.

4. **The `feedback_v3_engineered_feature_pivot.md` IC carve-out does NOT extend to coarser-frequency aggregations.** Per Critic Recommendation 1, the IC carve-out is scoped explicitly to **algebraically-composed Category 2 features** (`feature_A × sign(feature_B − threshold)`) that share variance with their primitives BY CONSTRUCTION — there is a formula relating them. A coarser-frequency aggregation of the same OHLCV is NOT algebraically composed from an 8h feature: there is no formula `d_ret_5d = f(8h features)`; the empirical 0.849 IC of `d_ret_5d` with `regime_momentum_signed_5d` is a Category 1 redundancy, and the strict |IC| < 0.70 family gate applies in full. The brief Section 4.4 stretched the carve-out to "different frequency of the same mechanism" — that stretch is rejected. Generalizable rule for any future multi-frequency axis: a coarser/finer-frequency transform of an existing primitive is a Category 1 feature; either ship only the orthogonal-by-IC subset, or request a formal skill amendment with explicit reviewer sign-off before invoking a carve-out for frequency transforms. The contested carve-out did not change the /113 verdict (NEGATIVE on C1 regardless) but it must not be re-invoked.
