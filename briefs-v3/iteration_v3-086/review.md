### Check 1 — Look-Ahead Audit: PASS

This is the central audit surface for /086 — a NEW crypto-native data feed (spot 8h klines) merged into the perp candle frame. Four sub-surfaces audited independently:

(a) **Spot→perp merge alignment.** `basis_v3.py:132-134` left-joins `spot_df[["open_time","close"]]` onto the perp frame on `open_time` with `how="left"`. A left-join means every perp row keeps its position; a missing spot candle yields NaN (LightGBM-native). (b) **Microsecond/millisecond normalisation.** `main.py::_to_ms` (line 1188-1195) divides by 1000 when `val >= 1e15`, applied to `parts[0]` and `parts[6]` of every archive row (lines 1224-1225) and to every REST kline (lines 1254-1255). The committed adversarial spike-perturbation test (`test_past_only_no_lookahead`, perturb `perp_close[250]` +50%, assert all basis columns at bars <250 bit-identical) passes; the EDA's independent T4 audit (`t4_past_only_audit.csv`, perturb bar K=400, all 3 symbols × 3 features = 9/9 `bars_before_K_identical = True`) corroborates.
