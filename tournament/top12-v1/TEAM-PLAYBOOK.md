# Top-12 V1 team playbook

## 1. Write before running

Preregister every candidate before market access. Declare the parent, hypothesis, falsifier,
formation and rebalance horizons, controls, neighborhood, material parameters, and tags. Changing
an executable byte or material declaration creates a new identity and trial.

## 2. Complete the research matrix

Each team receives twelve accepted trials:

1. transparent baseline;
2. exact sign inversion;
3. at least three economically distinct formation horizons;
4. at least two holding or rebalance horizons;
5. controls-off, individual-control, and combined-control ablations;
6. long, short, and chop role checks; and
7. a preregistered local neighborhood with at least five points.

Runs can satisfy several cells when declared prospectively. At least eight accepted trials and a
complete certificate are required before nomination. A failed or crashed run still consumes its
slot.

## 3. React to evidence

- RED: diagnose sign, horizon, neutrality, execution, and cost failure; do not submit.
- AMBER: make one narrow, preregistered repair aimed at the named weakness.
- GREEN: verify ablations, neighborhood, folds, concentration, and trial adjustment.
- Falsified family: request the single pivot or retire after mandatory evidence exists.

Parameter fishing is not research. Prefer plateaus, simple mechanisms, and low turnover. Every
control needs a prospective failure mode and a controls-off comparison.

## 4. Preserve blindness

Use only your own evidence. Do not search for comparable prior code or ask the organizer which
mechanisms have worked. A collision refusal reveals no conflicting strategy. Do not infer other
teams' progress from timing or organizer feedback.

## 5. Nominate once

The nominated candidate must pass every frozen IS gate and exactly match a successful source
archive. Nomination is terminal. After every team has nominated or validly retired, the organizer
ranks eligible nominees and freezes at most five.

## 6. Historical championship

Selected teams receive no 2024-07-01 through 2026-06-30 feedback until the atomic final release.
There is one observation, no retry, and no post-result repair.
