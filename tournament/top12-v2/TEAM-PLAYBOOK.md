# Top-12 V2 team playbook

Preregister every immutable candidate: parent, hypothesis, falsifier, horizons, controls,
neighborhood, parameters, and evidence tags. Accepted failures consume a trial.

Trials 1-8 must collectively cover baseline, exact sign inversion, three formation horizons, two
rebalance horizons, three control profiles, and role checks. Before trial 9, declare one numeric
local neighborhood. Trials 9-13 are exactly its center and four axial perturbations. Trials 14-16
are optional, narrow, prospectively tagged repairs. A nomination or valid retirement needs at
least thirteen trials and a certificate covering every accepted request.

Prefer stable plateaus, causal economic hypotheses, and low turnover. A strong aggregate metric
cannot repair a failed fold, regime, role, cost, concentration, or neighborhood gate. One mechanism
pivot is possible only after falsification and organizer approval before data access.

Use only your own lane and feedback. Never search for prior or peer strategies. A collision refusal
contains no information about the conflicting family. After nomination, there is no withdrawal,
repair, or feedback from sealed confirmation or OOS.
