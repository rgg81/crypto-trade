# Top-12 V2 robustness policy

Qualification is conjunctive. Six half-year development folds require at least five positive base
and 2x-return folds, worst-fold 2x Sharpe at least -0.25, and median-fold 2x Sharpe at least 0.75.
Full development Sharpe must be at least 1.00, annualized return at least 6%, 2x Sharpe at least
0.80, and 3x Sharpe above 0.25.

The 2,000-sample ten-day circular block bootstrap is adjusted for the complete accepted-trial
count; adjusted confidence must be at least 0.75. Annualized one-way turnover is capped at 18,
gross edge at 60 bps per turnover, and base cost share at 25%. Long and short contribution,
bull/bear/chop/stress behavior, positive-quarter breadth, and return concentration also gate.

Trials 9-13 form exactly one protected five-point neighborhood. At least four points must have
positive return and positive 2x Sharpe, its median 2x Sharpe must be at least 0.75, and every
declared coordinate must bracket the nominated value.

The sealed one-year confirmation is not tunable feedback. It requires positive base and 2x return,
base Sharpe at least 0.60, 2x Sharpe at least 0.50, drawdown at most 20%, three positive quarters,
turnover at most 18, edge at least 50 bps, and cost share at most 25%.
