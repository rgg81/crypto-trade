# Team isolation contract

A team may read only `TOURNAMENT-CHARTER-TOP12-V2.md`, `tournament/top12-v2/TEAM-API.md`, this
file, its own `tournament/top12-v2/teams/<team-id>/` lane, and standardized evidence returned for
its own accepted development trials.

It must not read prior tournament paths or results; `tournament/top12-v1`, `reports-top12-v1`, any
`top40` path, diaries or briefs; the organizer mechanism registry; another V2 team lane;
`reports-top12-v2`; private, journal, nomination, confirmation, selection, or OOS artifacts; git
history, branches, diffs, or objects used to discover strategies; or external descriptions of
strategies previously tested here. Repository search is not permission. Accidental exposure must
be reported immediately and not used.

The organizer checks current collisions and known mechanisms already observed on this OOS. A
refusal or reassignment never reveals the conflicting strategy. Teams must not ask which ideas or
parameters worked previously or infer peers' results from timing. Exposure quarantines the lane;
sealed-data exposure permanently ends the lineage.
