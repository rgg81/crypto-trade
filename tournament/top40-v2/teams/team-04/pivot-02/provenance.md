trades, with every reported return and Sharpe statistic equal to zero. The stopped UTC and BER
histories remain intact. No private qualifier, final OOS observation, other-team source or result,

hand-built exact timestamp grid before any score could exist. PARD makes timezone normalization and
actual completed-row ordering explicit, but its alpha is a distinct path-state mechanism rather
than a repaired BER shock sign.

three-day coherence blocks, fixed `0.60/0.40` trend mix, `0.35` trend floor, 48-hour schedule,
