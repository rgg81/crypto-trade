# Team 04 BER pivot provenance

BER is Team 04's first documented mechanism pivot after the exact UTC reference was stopped. The
only outcome knowledge used to motivate the pivot is Team 04's own visible-development review:
UTC had two flat early quarters, only seven positive quarters, concentrated fold performance, a
negative whole-window short sleeve before costs, and no persisted score suitable for its required
IC. No private/final observation, other V2 team, V1 result, ballot, or leaderboard was inspected.

The pivot changes the causal economic mechanism from multi-week continuation plus funding carry to
short-lived inventory-exhaustion reversal. The 21-day baseline, three-day shock, fixed coherence
multiplier, two-day schedule, 24-symbol minimum, broad 30-percent sleeves, 0.48 gross, and 0.03 cap
were specified before observing any BER target, trade, return, score IC, or symbol outcome.

The initial package included a team-owned score serializer designed with the strategy. After the
organizer selected the A5 identity-hook architecture—but before registration, testing, or any BER
evaluation—that serializer was explicitly demoted to a noncanonical audit-only artifact. It is not
promoted and is never an official score source.

The prospective canonical design imports organizer-owned
`crypto_trade.tournament.score_adapter_protocol_v5.score_boundary` and calls it on the exact ranked
dictionary after transformation and before selection. No claim is made that A5 currently exists or
is frozen. Registration remains blocked until its frozen authority and valid opt-in manifest bind
that hook and the canonical source bundle.

No script, test, evaluator, tournament CLI, Git command, or network request was run while creating
this pivot package. All commands in `validation_commands.json` and `test_evidence.json` are
serialized instructions for a later authorized reviewer and remain unexecuted. Hash and timestamp
fields remain explicit root-owned placeholders.
