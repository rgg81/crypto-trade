# Team 05 pivot-01 — Up/Down Capture Convexity

Status: prospective, unregistered, and without development evidence.

The parent CRTR family produced a positive no-control core, but its fixed combined-risk candidate
failed noncompensatory quarter, fold, confidence, chop, regime, and concentration gates. Controls
and neighbors cannot rescue that family. Pivot-01 therefore replaces the forecast object rather
than tuning CRTR.

UDCC measures structural conditional participation in the common native-crypto tape. For each
eligible contract it estimates a contemporaneous slope on positive common-tape bars and a separate
slope on negative common-tape bars over 252 closed eight-hour returns. High upside capture and low
downside capture is ranked long; the opposite asymmetry is ranked short. Observation order inside
the sample is irrelevant, future tape direction is never forecast, and no funding, volume, trend,
reversal, residual persistence, acceleration, diffusion, or lead-lag signal is used.

Exactly one weekly, dollar-balanced, no-control core is authorized. It must pass every gate in
`qualification_thresholds.json`. A failed core rejects the UDCC family immediately: controls,
sign flips, neighbors, and same-family replacement trials remain forbidden. Team 05's second
mechanism pivot is not consumed by this declaration and could be used only for a separately
documented genuinely different thesis.

Amendments 0005, 0006, and 0007 are active. Amendment 0008 is historical and supplies no authority
to UDCC. No private or OOS data may be accessed before formal development qualification.
