# Pivot 2 provenance: Funding Inventory Relaxation

FIR was formed from Team 02's three terminal experiment records and the public V2 methodology,
config, templates, and evaluator interfaces. No raw development artifact, market snapshot, other
team namespace, V1 team/result, private qualifier, final OOS observation, ballot, or leaderboard
was inspected. No evaluator, tournament CLI, Git command, commit, or shared journal/state mutation
was performed during formation or implementation.

The only result facts used were: C3RP baseline insolvency; the no-direction C3RP diagnostic's
negative aggregate, doubled-cost, bull, bear, and drawdown summary; and DAA reference insolvency.
Those facts enforce both prior stop rules and motivate lower structural exposure. They did not
select a timestamp, symbol, fitted coefficient, target, or outcome-dependent feature.

FIR's expected return is the realized funding transfer. This differs from C3RP, where funding was a
small crowding penalty on a residual price-return forecast, and from DAA, where OHLC location and
taker flow forecast directional price continuation. FIR has no price-return score; a fixed
realized-volatility exclusion is construction-only. Its 21/3-day windows, 75/25 rank blend,
four-fifths filter, 0.40 gross, 0.03 cap, and three-day schedule were fixed before any FIR result.

Three material trials and one pivot have been consumed. Formal registration of FIR would consume
the second and final pivot; evaluation of `team-02-fir-reference-001` would consume material trial
four. All unused trial budget remains governed by the staged 29-observation maximum and stop rule.
