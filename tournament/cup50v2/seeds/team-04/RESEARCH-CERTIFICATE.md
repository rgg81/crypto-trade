# team-04 research certificate

> Template. The organizer's seed ships with this unfilled on purpose: a certificate is a record of
> research that happened, and none has yet. Every section below must be answered in the team's own
> words before nomination, and the validator refuses a placeholder.

## 1. Mechanism and lane alignment

What the candidate trades, why that is this lane's question, and where it departs from the seed.

## 2. Pre-registered regime expectations

Written **before the second charged trial** and timestamped by the research ledger. State the
expected sign in bull, bear, chop and high-dispersion markets, and say which of those would falsify
the mechanism if it came out the other way.

## 3. Experiment ledger

Research evaluations run (the ledger counts them; state the number and what you learned), then every
charged trial with its purpose and what it changed.

## 4. Falsifiers and ablations

Sign inversion, the seed-versus-candidate delta, and at least one ablation removing a component the
mechanism is claimed to need. Report what happened, including when it was inconvenient.

## 5. Cost

Turnover, cost as a share of gross, and where the candidate sits at 3x. The cost wall, not signal
absence, is what kills most books here.

## 6. Risk declaration rationale

Why that set of controls, and what it costs when it is wrong.

## 7. Known weaknesses

The regime, the market state, or the data condition under which you expect this to be worst. A
certificate that lists none is not finished.
