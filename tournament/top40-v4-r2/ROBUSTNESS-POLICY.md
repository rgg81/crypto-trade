# Robustness policy

Qualification is conjunctive. Aggregate performance never compensates for a failed fold, cost,
role, concentration, neighborhood, or trial-adjusted confidence gate.

- Five fixed chronological folds cover 2020 through 2024H1; at least four must be positive at
  base and 2x costs.
- Base Sharpe must be at least 1.0, 2x Sharpe at least 0.75, and 3x Sharpe positive.
- Annualized turnover is capped at 20x; gross edge must be at least 50 bps per one-way turnover;
  base costs may consume at most 25% of positive gross PnL.
- Bull, bear, and chop Sharpe must be positive; stress Sharpe must be at least -0.50; long and
  short gross contribution must each be positive.
- The largest five absolute-return days may contribute at most 35% of absolute daily return and no
  fold may contribute more than 60% of positive arithmetic PnL.
- A five-point preregistered local neighborhood must vary every coordinate both directions, pass
  at least 70% of points, and have median 2x Sharpe at least 0.50.
- Sign-inversion evidence must cite its baseline as parent and is accepted only when immutable
  targets are the exact negative on the complete decision grid under the same risk policy.
- A 2,000-sample, 10-day circular-block bootstrap is adjusted for the team's complete accepted
  trial count. Adjusted confidence must be at least 0.90.
- At close, a second family-wise adjustment uses the total accepted-trial count across all lanes;
  field-adjusted confidence must also be at least 0.90 before advancement.

Six finalists is a ceiling, not a target. Floors remain fixed if fewer qualify.
