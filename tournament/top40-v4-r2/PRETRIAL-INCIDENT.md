# Pretrial executor incident

The first Team-01 discovery process on 2026-08-20 stopped before creating a candidate or outbox.
Codex CLI 0.148.0 applied `--ignore-user-config` after security overrides supplied at the global
layer, rebuilt its configuration, and entered `read-only` instead of the named permission profile.
The broker accepted no trial, retirement, nomination, feedback, or score; the research journal
remained byte-empty.

The frozen v8 command places `exec --strict-config --ignore-user-config` before every permission,
approval, network, and disabled-capability override. A lease-serialized organizer smoke session
then reported `sandbox: custom permissions` and `approval: never`, created exactly the requested
lane-local sentinel, and left no durable lane delta after verified cleanup. Independent physical
probes separately verified allowed kit/lane reads, own-lane write, peer/root/holdout denial,
network denial, and host-process hiding.

Recovery is deliberately limited to this exact pretrial state. It requires the known old activation
file, activation record, activation test output, and v7 Team-01 discovery authority by SHA-256;
an empty journal; all 15 seed-only lanes; and no report, source archive, selection, nomination, or
release artifact. The old activation is archived first. A fresh committed v8 activation must then
validate before the stale v7 launch pointer is archived. Only the final content-addressed incident
record removes the recovery-pending launch block.
