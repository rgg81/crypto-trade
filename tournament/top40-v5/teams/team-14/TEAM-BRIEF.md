# team-14 — breadth and market state

**Economic family:** event and state

## Your mandate

Time net exposure from cross-sectional breadth and dispersion, so the field contains at least one lane taking a view on market state.

## Your falsifier

State this on visible development data before you look at a result. A mandate that cannot be wrong is not a mandate.

> If breadth does not lead index returns, it is a coincident summary rather than a state variable.

## What is fixed

- Your first charged trial is the **unmodified organizer seed**. The leaderboard reports how far your nomination moved from it.
- The mandate names a family, never a parameter, an implementation or an expected sign. How you express it is yours.
- You may not target volatility yourself. A common ex-ante risk unit scales every book to the same ex-ante volatility, so all lanes are compared at equal risk.

- **You are mandated to run non-zero net exposure** inside the |net| ≤ 0.25 cap. A market-neutral book does not satisfy this mandate.
