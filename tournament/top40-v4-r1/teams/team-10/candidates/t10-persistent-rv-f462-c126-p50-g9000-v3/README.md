# Team 10 persistent-correlation relative value: 462-bar formation

This candidate is the 462-bar point in the fully frozen 315, 350, 378, 420, and 462 bar
formation grid. It requires pair correlation of at least 0.72 in the latest 126 completed returns
and at least 0.50 in the immediately preceding 126 returns, ranks neighbors by their weaker window,
and keeps the hedge, convergence, entry, 0.90 gross, weekly schedule, and full controls fixed.

The point tests whether the persistent relationship and its spread convergence survive a modest
change in level-fit history. The neighborhood is falsified if fewer than 70% of points have positive
annual return and doubled-cost Sharpe, median doubled-cost Sharpe is below 0.50, or no bracketed
interior point clears every finalist gate. This candidate makes no performance claim and uses only
completed pure-crypto bars outside the sealed OOS interval.
