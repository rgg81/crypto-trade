# Team 10 persistent-correlation relative value: formation center

This candidate replaces the over-strict Engle–Granger p-value screen with a simpler structural
stability gate. A candidate neighbor must have return correlation of at least 0.72 in the most
recent 126 completed returns and at least 0.50 in the immediately preceding 126 returns. Neighbors
are ranked by their weaker-window correlation. The bounded hedge, AR half-life, crossing,
dislocation, weekly schedule, and full organizer controls otherwise remain unchanged.

Because the baseline realized only 0.0286 average gross, 3.78 annual turnover, and 3.21% drawdown,
the repaired book raises its target cap from 0.30 to 0.90 gross while preserving proportional
symbol and net limits below the tournament maxima. This is a declared risk-utilization change, not
leverage chosen from a repair result.

This is the center of `t10-persistent-rv-formation-n1`. Before any point is evaluated, the complete
formation grid is frozen at 315, 350, 378, 420, and 462 completed 8-hour bars; persistent-correlation
windows, thresholds, entry rule, scale, schedule, and controls remain identical. The neighborhood
is falsified if fewer than 70% of points have positive annual return and doubled-cost Sharpe, its
median doubled-cost Sharpe is below 0.50, or no bracketed interior point clears every finalist gate.

Every decision uses completed pure-crypto bars and next-open execution. The candidate makes no
performance claim and cannot access the sealed OOS interval.
