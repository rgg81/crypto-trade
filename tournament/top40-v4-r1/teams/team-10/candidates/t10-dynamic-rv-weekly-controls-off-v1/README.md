# Team 10 dynamic relative value: controls-off ablation

This diagnostic keeps the baseline pair graph, rolling hedge fits, convergence gates, spread
scaling, weekly schedule, and 0.30 gross cap unchanged. It disables every optional organizer
control: volatility targeting, drawdown brakes, position stops, time stops, and the turnover
throttle.

The baseline is already sparse and low-turnover, but its 2020 and 2022 folds and bear regime are
negative. This ablation tests whether close-confirmed controls interrupt genuine convergence or
whether the weakness belongs to unstable pair relationships. It is falsified if bear and fold
breadth do not improve, or if unprotected drawdown, turnover, costs, or role balance deteriorate.

This candidate is outside the finalist neighborhood and makes no performance claim. Every input is
a completed pure-crypto bar and the sealed OOS interval remains inaccessible.
