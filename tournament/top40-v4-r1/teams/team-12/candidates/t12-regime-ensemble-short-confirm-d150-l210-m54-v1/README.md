residual momentum, short-horizon residual reversal, and downside resilience. Component weights are
fixed for bull, bear, chop, and stress states. The router derives direction and volatility from BTC

names exist. Chop and stress never force a short hedge. Short gross is capped by both the router
budget and the per-symbol limit; the unused budget remains cash rather than enlarging longs. All
optional central controls remain disabled.

This is a preregistered interior-low neighborhood point, not a performance claim. The local grid
varies BTC direction, full-history, and momentum horizons while preserving the causal lag, component
set, fixed regime weights, and directional short-confirmation rule.
