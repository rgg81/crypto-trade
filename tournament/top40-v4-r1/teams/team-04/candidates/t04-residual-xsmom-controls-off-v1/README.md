# Team 04 residual cross-sectional momentum: controls-off ablation

This diagnostic candidate keeps the baseline strategy byte-for-byte unchanged. It estimates each
coin's beta to the median native-crypto return, ranks volatility-normalized eighty-four-day
residual momentum after a seven-day skip, and refreshes balanced top and bottom cohorts weekly.

All optional organizer controls are disabled. The baseline logged 1,105 position-stop actions,
621 drawdown-brake actions, 373 blocks, and 187 time stops while realizing only 0.153 average gross
from a 0.44 target. This preregistered run determines whether those interventions truncate relative
trends or whether the 2021/2022 and short-sleeve weakness belongs to the raw signal.

The ablation is falsified if fold breadth and doubled-cost Sharpe do not improve, or if unprotected
drawdown, turnover, or cost sensitivity becomes unacceptable. It is diagnostic, outside the final
neighborhood, and makes no performance claim.
