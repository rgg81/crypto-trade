# Team 02 crypto-breadth-gated weekly breakout: neighborhood center

This candidate makes one narrow repair to the preceding dual-trend trial. Instead of requiring
each coin's fast score to agree with its own slow return, it derives a causal native-crypto regime
from the fraction of eligible coins with a positive completed sixty-three-day return. At breadth
of at least 55%, only positive fast-breakout scores are eligible; at breadth of at most 45%, only
negative scores are eligible; inside the neutral band, both sleeves may trade.

The twenty-day channel, seven-day return confirmation, persistence rule, 0.36 gross budget, weekly
Monday schedule, and risk policy remain unchanged. The sole organizer overlay is a 22% volatility
target. The regime uses no stablecoin, TradFi, metal, index, hindsight date, or OOS observation.

This is the preregistered center of `t02-breadth-gated-weekly-neighborhood`. It directly addresses
the prior run's aggregate counter-regime losses: shorts during broad bull years and longs during
the 2022 bear. It is falsified if Sharpe, quarter breadth, and confidence do not improve while long,
short, and neutral-regime roles remain positive. It makes no performance claim.
