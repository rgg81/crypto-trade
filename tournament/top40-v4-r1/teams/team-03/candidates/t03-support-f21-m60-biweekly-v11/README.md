# Team 03 biweekly rebalance control

This candidate is identical to the trial-3 full-control center except for scheduling. It refreshes targets every fourteen days on deterministic alternate Mondays anchored at 2020-01-06 00:00 UTC. The 21-day trend, 14-day quote-volume support, 60-day BTC state, exposure budgets, and risk policy are unchanged.

The point supplies the required second rebalance horizon and tests whether the edge survives slower trading rather than relying on weekly membership churn.
