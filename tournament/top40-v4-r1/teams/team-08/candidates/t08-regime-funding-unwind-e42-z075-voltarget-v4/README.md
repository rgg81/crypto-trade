# Team 08 selective funding-event center point

This candidate restores the fresher three-day confirmed-unwind and seven-day chop cohorts, then
raises the minimum absolute funding z-score from 0.50 to 0.75 outside chop and from 1.25 to 1.50
inside chop. The funding lookback, confirmation, selection, exposure, and volatility overlay stay
fixed.

It is the center of a preregistered five-point neighborhood over funding-reference length and the
unwind z threshold. The test asks whether rejecting marginal crowding events can cross the
turnover and edge-density gates without sacrificing fresh-signal PnL or either sleeve role.
