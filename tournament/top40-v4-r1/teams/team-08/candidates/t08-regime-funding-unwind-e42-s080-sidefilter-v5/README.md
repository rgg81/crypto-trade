# Team 08 causal regime-side filter center

The trial-3 attribution shows that funding-unwind shorts have low gross edge in BTC bull states
and negative gross PnL in high-volatility stress, while bear/chop shorts and protected longs remain
profitable. This candidate therefore keeps both sides in bear and ordinary chop, but suppresses
short targets whenever the causal sixty-day BTC state is bull or trailing thirty-day BTC
volatility exceeds 80% annualized.

Funding standardization, confirmation, selection, three/seven-day cohorts, exposure limits, and
the volatility-only organizer overlay remain fixed. This is the center of a preregistered local
neighborhood over funding-reference length and the stress-volatility threshold.
