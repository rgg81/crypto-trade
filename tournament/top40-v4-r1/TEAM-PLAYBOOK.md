# Top-40 V4 team playbook

## 1. Write before running

Every candidate directory contains an immutable `candidate.json`, `strategy.py`, `risk_policy.json`
and any candidate-local modules. Before a run, declare the parent, hypothesis, falsifier, formation
and rebalance horizons, control profile, neighborhood group, material parameters and research tags.
Changing any executable byte or material declaration creates a new candidate and trial.

## 2. Complete the research matrix

Each team receives twelve accepted trials. Use them deliberately:

1. transparent baseline;
2. exact sign inversion;
3. at least three economically distinct formation horizons;
4. at least two holding/rebalance horizons;
5. controls-off, individual-control and combined-control ablations;
6. long, short and chop role checks; and
7. a preregistered local neighborhood with at least five points around the proposed finalist.

Runs can satisfy several cells when declared prospectively. At least eight accepted trials and a
complete certificate are required before nomination. A failed/crashed run still consumes its slot.

## 3. React to evidence

- RED: diagnose sign, horizon, neutrality, execution and cost failure; do not submit.
- AMBER: make one narrow, preregistered repair aimed at the named weakness.
- GREEN: verify ablations, neighborhood, folds, concentration and trial-adjusted confidence.
- Falsified family: use the single documented pivot or retire after the mandatory evidence exists.

Parameter fishing is not research. Prefer plateaus, simple mechanisms and slow turnover. Every
control needs a failure mode and a controls-off comparison.

## 4. Nominate once

The nominated candidate must pass every frozen IS gate and exactly match a successful source
archive. Nomination is terminal. After every team has nominated or validly retired, the organizer
ranks eligible nominees and freezes at most five.

## 5. Historical championship

Selected teams receive no 2024-07-01 through 2026-06-30 feedback until the atomic final release.
There is one observation, no retry and no post-result repair. The official winner is determined by
the charter's 2x-cost OOS ranking after winner-eligibility gates.

## 6. Research certificate

The certificate lists the exact successful or terminal journal records satisfying each mandatory
cell. The organizer verifies candidate IDs, tags, horizons, control profiles and neighborhood
membership against the append-only journal. Missing evidence cannot be replaced by prose.

## 7. Before every candidate run

- Confirm pure-crypto point-in-time eligibility and causal fields.
- Confirm deterministic output under the frozen seed.
- Confirm targets execute at the next open and never use future rows.
- Confirm risk controls use close-confirmed state and next-open execution.
- Confirm base, 2x and 3x costs are understood.
- Confirm candidate metadata and parent are accurate.
- Confirm the run is worth consuming one of twelve trials.
