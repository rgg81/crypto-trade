# Team-02 QR brief — Directed Lagged Diffusion Network

**Candidate:** `t02-dldn-c01`. **Hypothesis:** after an idiosyncratic move by a statistically established upstream contract, related contracts incompletely incorporate the information over the next 8–24 hours; a causal directed network can trade the remaining response gap.

## Frozen mechanism

At decision boundary `t`, `P_i(t)` is the transaction close of the 8h candle ending at `t`; it is available at `t`. Define `r_i(t)=log(P_i(t)/P_i(t-8h))`. Targets decided at `t` fill at the hidden transaction open timestamped `t`. The training label is `y_i(t)=log(P_i(t+8h)/P_i(t))`, interval `(t,t+8h]`; training labels must end by the current decision.

Refit at Monday 00:00 UTC and whenever `tuple(context.eligible_symbols)` differs from the immediately preceding tuple. At refit `T`, use that tuple `E` and exactly the union grid `U={T-(L-1)8h,...,T}`; `r_i(s)` is missing unless both required closes exist. Set `m(s)` to the median of finite `r_j(s), j in E` (missing if none). For each asset, fit intercept OLS `r_i=alpha_i+beta_i*m+u_i` on exactly its finite `(r_i,m)` rows via Moore-Penrose `rcond=1e-10`; require `max(180,ceil(.6L))` rows and finite coefficients. Compute residual center and `1.4826*MAD` on those same rows, floored at `1e-4`, and `z_i=clip((u_i-center)/scale,-4,4)`. The label-origin grid is `U` without `T`; labels are `z_i(s+8h)` under these same fitted parameters.

For each follower `i`, fit ridge

`z_i(s+8h)=a_i+phi_i*z_i(s)+sum_{j!=i} b_ji*z_j(s)+epsilon`,

with unpenalized intercept and penalty `lambda` on every slope; solve float64 normal equations with `pinv(rcond=1e-10)` and penalty diagonal `(0,lambda,...)`. For follower `i`, its full rows are label origins with finite response; impute every missing predictor, including own lag, to zero. Sort those rows by origin and split the first `floor(n/2)` versus the remainder; fit full and both halves on their exact rows. For edge `j->i`, overlap counts only rows where `z_j(s)` was actually finite and the response finite; require `max(160,ceil(.5L))` full and 80 in each half. Keep identical nonzero coefficient signs, `abs(b_full)>=tau`; rank by descending `abs(b_full)`, breaking ties by leader symbol, and retain `K`. Reliability is `q=min(abs(b_h1),abs(b_h2))/max(abs(b_h1),abs(b_h2),1e-12)`; set `v_ji=sign(b_full)*max(abs(b_full)-tau,0)*q`, then row-normalize by `sum(abs(v))`. Confidence is `c_i=mean(q)*min(1,n_label/(L-1))`; at prediction, missing leader `z` is zero and missing follower `z` makes that follower unscored. Then `g_i=c_i*(sum_j v_ji*z_j(t)-kappa*z_i(t))`.

Set `A_ii=0`, `A_ij=abs(v_ij)+abs(v_ji)`, degree `d_i=sum_j A_ij`, and linked set `d_i>0`; unlinked valid assets always receive zero. Fewer than 12 valid or linked assets flattens. Use 2/3/4 communities for 12–14/15–19/20+ linked assets. Form `Lsym=I-D^(-1/2)AD^(-1/2)` and take eigenvectors for the smallest eigenvalues (ascending), row-normalized to unit Euclidean length. First k-means center is the lexicographically smallest symbol; each next maximizes distance to its nearest selected center (lexical tie). Lloyd uses squared Euclidean distance, lowest cluster-index ties, arithmetic-mean centers, and stops on unchanged assignments or 50 iterations. An empty cluster takes the lexically tied farthest point from its assigned center among clusters with size above one, processed by cluster index.

At each decision compute `u_i` using fixed refit `alpha,beta` and the latest 90 union timestamps ending at the decision; `sigma_i` is finite-`u_i` sample standard deviation (`ddof=1`), requiring 45. A linked asset lacking current `z` or valid sigma gets zero and is excluded from projection. Among the remainder, floor sigma by `max(1e-4, 20th percentile)` using linear interpolation. Set `a_i=g_i/sigma_i`; use `s_a=1.4826*MAD(a)` and clip to `median(a)+/-4s_a`; `s_a<=1e-12` flattens. Project off all community dummies and beta demeaned within community: `h=a-F pinv(F'F)F'a`, with Moore-Penrose `rcond=1e-10`. Singleton communities therefore receive zero. Rank deficiency uses that pseudoinverse; nonfinite eigensystems/projections, row norms `<=1e-12`, or `sum(abs(h))<=1e-12` flatten. Otherwise `G=min(.80,.075*sum(abs(h))/max(abs(h)))`, `w_i=G*h_i/sum(abs(h))`.

Compute desired weights every 8h. Every Monday and every eligible-tuple change triggers one refit and a forced explicit mapping; invalid refits force `{}`. Any invalid between-refit score also returns `{}`. After any `{}`, set `w_last` to the empty mapping; after a nonempty mapping set it to exactly that mapping; `None` leaves it unchanged. On other valid decisions, compute distance over the union of desired and `w_last` symbols with absent weights zero: return `None` if distance `<H`, otherwise the full mapping. Central exits and reductions remain active.
