## Equal budgets and gates

An incumbent is an ordinary material V3 trial from its first train request. It
uses the owning team's normal append-only trial journal, its three opening
validation probes, any two comeback probes for which the team later qualifies,
and its single formal nomination. Grandfathering creates no extra run, probe,
nomination, priority slot, or exception to causal, integrity, solvency, execution,
and pure-crypto rules.

No incumbent is automatically green, readiness-eligible, public-core eligible,
privately qualified, or final-OOS eligible. Before nomination, its exact V3 bytes
must independently produce strictly positive net Sharpe, annualized return, and
doubled-cost Sharpe on training and on sealed validation, pass every ordinary
hard gate and public-core floor, and match the candidate that consumed the probe.
Historical V2 metrics are calibration evidence only.

## Challenger discipline

Teams 04, 05, 07, and 09 may develop challengers in the same laboratory. The
incumbent is the team's historical benchmark, not a mandatory final choice. A
challenger replaces it as the team's preferred lane only when standardized,
logged V3 evidence supports the change—for example, a repaired qualification
gap, stronger cost and regime robustness, lower drawdown or concentration, or a
matching positive validation packet. Novelty or a persuasive narrative is not
replacement evidence. This workflow rule adds no hidden performance gate: the
charter's fixed gates and ranking remain authoritative.

If evidence is incomplete or mixed, the team may keep both candidates alive
within the same shared budgets. It may still lock only one nominee, and the lock
must be the exact candidate whose own train and validation evidence satisfies all
nomination rules. A challenger does not inherit the incumbent's probe or result,
and the incumbent does not inherit a challenger's result.
