# resid-composite-s18

`resid-composite` with `SHORT_SLEEVE_FRACTION` at 0.18 instead of 0.20, and nothing else changed.

The question this answers was posed before it ran: the short sleeve is the floor with the least
margin in this lane -- `short_gross_pnl` measured +0.017 at a fifth on trial #32 and +0.093 at a
fifth with the composite score on trial #33, against a long sleeve of +0.49 and +0.57 -- and the
in-sample surface says the margin is still improving as the sleeve narrows. If it is, the sleeve
width is not yet at the point where narrowing costs more spread than it buys in absolute decline;
if it is not, 0.20 is the right width and this run says so.
