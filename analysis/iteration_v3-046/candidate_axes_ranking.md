## Direction asymmetry — the key finding

The diagnostic uncovered SEVERE direction asymmetry on BCH and TRX:

**BCH (n=94 IS):**
- LONG: 39 trades, 30.8% WR, **-25.07% net_pnl** (toxic)
- SHORT: 55 trades, 43.6% WR, **+48.69% net_pnl** (positive)
- BCH IS = +23.62 = +48.69 (SHORT) − 25.07 (LONG)

**TRX (n=85 IS):**
- LONG: 42 trades, 42.9% WR, **+24.77% net_pnl** (positive)
- SHORT: 43 trades, 25.6% WR, **-32.05% net_pnl** (catastrophic)
- TRX IS = -7.28 = +24.77 (LONG) − 32.05 (SHORT)

**BCH LONGs and TRX SHORTs are the IS bottleneck.**

## Five Candidate Axes (Ranked)

### #1 — BCH per-symbol ATR widening (2.0, 1.5) [RECOMMENDED]

**Mechanism**: Mirror iter-v3/044 ALGO + iter-v3/045 LDO mechanism. Wider SL gives BCH LONGs
more headroom before stop-out.

**Why BCH and not TRX**: BCH IS=OOS SL:TP=1.93 is stable across both windows — SL widening
helps SYMMETRICALLY (lifts both IS AND OOS). TRX OOS SL:TP=0.96 already better than IS=2.24,
so SL widening risks HURTING TRX OOS by widening losses on already-working SHORTs.

**IS lift estimate**:
- BCH IS SL count = 56 of 94 trades (mean_SL = -3.90%).
- If 1.5×SL compresses SL rate by 10pp (similar to LDO mechanism), ~9 SL trades become
  TP/timeout. Net swing: 9 × (-3.90 + 5.0) = +10 to +12% IS lift on BCH.
- Bundle IS lift estimate: +0.10 to +0.18 IS Sharpe (BCH is 64% of bundle IS PnL).

**OOS impact**:
- BCH OOS SL pattern matches IS — SL widening helps OOS too (~+5% OOS lift).
- ALGO/LDO/TRX bit-identical (per-symbol Optuna independence).
- Bundle OOS Sharpe lift estimate: +0.05 to +0.15.

**Pre-registered classification thresholds**:
- PATH A: BCH OOS PnL ≥ +5% AND BCH+TRX+ALGO+LDO non-BCH bit-identical AND bundle OOS
  Sharpe Δ ≥ -0.10 vs iter-v3/045 anchor.
- PATH B-MECHANICAL: BCH trades bit-identical to iter-v3/045 (Falsifier 1 fires).
- PATH C: BCH OOS PnL < -5% OR bundle OOS Sharpe Δ < -0.20 vs iter-v3/045.

**Compoundability**: STACKABLE with iter-v3/044 ALGO + iter-v3/045 LDO at iter-v3/050
CONFIRMATION (3 wider-SL applications → triple-validated mechanism). Architecturally clean.

**Risk**: 3-symbol stacking of per-symbol customizations exceeds iter-v3/039 scope (which had
1 feature + 1 ATR per-symbol). Multi-seed COULD replicate the IS-divergence pattern. Mitigation:
all 3 are symmetric labeling-layer mechanisms (no feature-layer changes), and the BCH
mechanism is BOTH IS and OOS positive (vs LDO which is OOS-strong / IS-modest).

**Implementation cost**: 1 line change in `V3_ATR_MULTIPLIERS_PER_SYMBOL` dict.

### #2 — TRX per-symbol ATR widening (2.0, 1.5)

**Mechanism**: Same as #1 applied to TRX. TRX IS SL:TP=2.24 highest in the bundle.

**Why ranked below BCH**: TRX OOS SL:TP=0.96 already much better than IS — SL widening
helps IS but RISKS hurting OOS. TRX IS net_pnl is already negative (-7.28%) so any IS lift
is welcome, but bundle OOS could regress.

**IS lift estimate**:
- TRX IS SL count = 56 of 85 (mean_SL = -2.16%, smaller absolute than BCH).
- If 1.5×SL compresses SL rate by 8pp (smaller than LDO because TRX signal is weaker),
  ~6 SL trades redirect: net swing 6 × (-2.16 + 3.0) = +5% IS lift on TRX.
- Bundle IS lift estimate: +0.05 to +0.10 IS Sharpe.

**OOS impact**:
- HIGH RISK of OOS regression. TRX OOS works because SL is tight (SL:TP=0.96). Widening SL
  could let losing trades absorb more loss without recovery.
- Bundle OOS Sharpe Δ estimate: -0.10 to -0.30 (HARMFUL).

**Verdict**: Address TRX via a different axis (direction filter, see #3). Do not widen SL.

### #3 — BCH LONG direction filter (suppress LONG signals on BCH)

**Mechanism**: Add a runtime gate that suppresses LONG signals on BCHUSDT only. Architecturally
novel for v3 (no prior direction filter).

**IS lift estimate**:
- Suppressing all 39 BCH LONG trades eliminates -25.07% IS PnL drag.
- Bundle IS lift estimate: +0.20 to +0.30 IS Sharpe (HIGH leverage).

**OOS impact**:
- Eliminates BCH LONG OOS drag (-7.44%, 28.6% WR — also toxic OOS).
- Bundle OOS Sharpe Δ estimate: +0.05 to +0.15.

**Architectural risk**:
- NOVEL mechanism — no v3 precedent for direction-side filtering.
- Could indicate the LightGBM model is mis-calibrated on BCH LONGs; suppressing the bad side
  is a bandage, not a fix. The proper fix would be to train BCH SHORT-only model
  (which is more invasive).
- Cycle 3 plan §Axis 5 forbids per-symbol customizations without IS-axis pre-validation;
  this IS the IS-axis evidence, but the mechanism is structurally different from feature+label
  customization.
- Could trigger PATH C if it dispatches incorrectly (suppression at runtime vs label-time).

**Implementation cost**: Modest (5-15 lines: dispatch in strategy or risk gate, plus
configuration field, plus tests).

**Why ranked #3 not #1**: Architectural novelty is high-risk in EXPLORATION budget.
Rank #1 (BCH per-symbol ATR) achieves similar IS lift (+0.10-0.18 vs +0.20-0.30) with
known mechanism + known dispatch path. Rank #3 reserves itself for a future iteration if #1
under-delivers.

### #4 — TRX SHORT direction filter (suppress SHORT signals on TRX)

**Mechanism**: Same as #3 applied to TRX SHORTs.

**IS lift estimate**:
- Suppressing all 43 TRX SHORT trades eliminates -32.05% IS PnL drag.
- Bundle IS lift estimate: +0.25 to +0.35 IS Sharpe (HIGHEST single-axis leverage).

**OOS impact**:
- TRX SHORT OOS is +8.39% (POSITIVE). Suppressing it removes +8 OOS PnL.
- Bundle OOS Sharpe Δ estimate: -0.05 to -0.15 (modest OOS regression).

**Why ranked #4 not #1**:
- TRX SHORT OOS is positive (+41.7% WR), so suppressing it is RIGHT for IS but WRONG for OOS.
  This is the iter-v3/039 anti-pattern: lift OOS at IS expense (here, lift IS at OOS expense).
- Architectural novelty same as #3.
- Risk-adjusted, this axis violates the strict BOTH-must-improve rule by design.

### #5 — ALGO ATR REVERT to default (2.0, 1.0)

**Mechanism**: Revert iter-v3/044 ALGO per-symbol ATR.

**IS lift estimate**:
- ALGO IS @ iter-v3/043 (default ATR) = -57.66%; @ iter-v3/045 (per-symbol ATR (2.0, 1.5)) = -34.05%.
- REVERT would worsen ALGO IS by -23.61pp — i.e., NEGATIVE IS lift.

**OOS impact**:
- Lose iter-v3/044's +49% OOS swing on ALGO (entire mechanism contribution).
- Bundle OOS Sharpe Δ estimate: -0.50 to -0.80 (CATASTROPHIC).

**Verdict**: REJECTED. Diagnostic showed ALGO ATR (2.0, 1.5) IMPROVED IS by +24%, not
regressed. Reverting would hurt BOTH IS and OOS.

## Recommendation

**iter-v3/046 axis = BCH per-symbol ATR (2.0, 1.5)** — `V3_ATR_MULTIPLIERS_PER_SYMBOL["BCHUSDT"] = (2.0, 1.5)`.

Rationale (in order of priority):
1. **Triple-application of validated mechanism** (ALGO at iter-v3/044, LDO at iter-v3/045, BCH at iter-v3/046). Pattern is well-understood; lowest architectural risk.
2. **BCH IS=OOS SL:TP=1.93 is stable** — wider SL helps IS AND OOS symmetrically (vs TRX which would help IS but hurt OOS).
3. **Highest IS lift among low-risk axes** (+0.10 to +0.18 IS Sharpe estimate; mechanism known to work).
4. **Implementation cost minimal** (1 dict entry + tests update).
5. **Compoundable at iter-v3/050 CONFIRMATION** with ALGO ATR + LDO ATR (3-symbol per-symbol-ATR validation, all wider-SL pattern).

Direction-filter axes (#3, #4) reserved for future iterations if mechanical ATR axes
saturate. ALGO REVERT (#5) rejected based on the IS regression diagnostic (which falsified
the initial hypothesis).
