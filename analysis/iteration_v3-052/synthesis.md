## Headline finding — premise REQUIRES CORRECTION

**The orchestrator brief's premise that "LDO is an IS drag" is FALSE.** The −14.96% number comes from `net_pnl_pct` aggregation in `per_symbol.csv`, which sums per-trade raw % returns and ignores the `weight_factor` (which encodes vol scaling and BTC-kill drops). The actual contribution to bundle Sharpe is the **weighted_pnl** (weight_factor × pnl_pct).

| Window | Direction | n | wins | WR | wpnl total | avg wpnl | SLs | TPs |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

**LDO OOS drag is concentrated in 11 SHORT trades** (vs 2 LONG trades). The model decided to short LDO 11 times during the OOS — and 9 of those got stop-lossed. The 2 OOS LONG trades have 50% WR and +2.49 avg wpnl (net positive). Critically: LDO went from $1.04 → $0.41 in OOS (−60% drawdown over 14 months) — so the SHORT direction was macro-correct, but the model's short timing kept landing in local-rally retraces and tripped SLs before the down-leg resumed.

**This is a direction-asymmetric failure pattern**: LDO LONGs work fine (4.99 wpnl, 50% WR over 2 trades); LDO SHORTs are catastrophic (−22.42 wpnl, 18.2% WR over 11 trades). A direction-asymmetric kill switch (LDO SHORT block, analogous to primitive 10 for BCH LONG) would surgically remove the −22.42 OOS drag while preserving the +4.99 LONG contribution — and would NOT regress the IS contribution if the IS LDO SHORTs (+5.57 wpnl across 3 trades) are also blocked at training time (model would adjust during Optuna re-tune).

This is the **structurally correct alternative axis** to LDO outright removal — and it's a different cycle 4 axis category (per-symbol direction-asymmetric kill switch, NOT universe contraction).
