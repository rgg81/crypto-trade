## Candidate 1 — Per-symbol ADX threshold (TRX 21) (RECOMMENDED)

**Axis classification**: F.1 (per-symbol regime gate variant — NEW gate-config field
parallel to existing `block_long_for`, `regime_gate_symbols`)

**Mechanism**: Add `adx_threshold_per_symbol: dict[str, float] = {}` field to
`RiskV2Config`. Currently `adx_threshold` is GLOBAL across all 4 symbols. The new
field allows per-symbol overrides; symbols not in the dict fall back to the global
value. iter-v3/049 sets `adx_threshold_per_symbol = {"TRXUSDT": 21.0}` (only TRX
gets the raise; BCH/LDO/ALGO unchanged at 20.0).

**Quantitative basis (with primitive 10 carry-forward simulation)**:
- TRX IS trades at ADX 20-21 (existing global threshold to candidate per-symbol
  threshold): n=9, all weighted_pnl<0, collective -4.77 wpnl.
- TRX OOS trades at same ADX range: n=2, collective -0.01 wpnl (effectively zero).
- IS lift: +4.77 wpnl (~5.1% of post-primitive-10 IS baseline 94.07).
- OOS preservation: 100.0% (-0.01 cost is negligible).
- **BOTH-must-improve PASSES** at the EDA stage per
  `feedback_v3_strict_both_is_oos_baseline.md`.

(All numbers from `axis_e_with_primitive10_carry.py`; see also
`axis_e_finegrained_adx_with_primitive10.csv`.)

**Implementation complexity**: LOW
- Add 1 field to `RiskV2Config` (~3 LOC).
- Modify `RiskV2Wrapper._adx_too_low` to consult per-symbol dict (~5 LOC).
- Wire in `run_baseline_v3.py:_build_v3_model` (~3 LOC).
- Update `_verify_feature_columns` for the new field (~10 LOC).
- 5 adversarial tests in `tests/strategies/ml/test_per_symbol_adx_threshold.py`.
- No feature regeneration required (gate-only change).
- Estimated setup time: 30-45 min; backtest 25-40 min; total ≤1.5h.

**Risks**:
- Naive counterfactual ignores Optuna's response. The model will retrain at
  higher TRX ADX threshold and the trade roster will SHIFT — actual effect
  depends on how Optuna fills the gap.
- **Primitive 10 carry-forward state mandatory**: the EDA finding holds ONLY
  with primitive 10 ON (without primitive 10, BCH ADX 21 would also lift IS).
  The naive counterfactual without primitive 10 (Strategy A in
  `axis_e_per_symbol_adx_simulator.py`) shows +20.22 IS / -0.17 OOS, but with
  primitive 10 ON only the TRX component survives the BOTH-must-improve check.

  symbol cap CLOSED at /020, BTC regime gate CLOSED at /022, primitive 10
  ADVANCED at /047). The axis-saturation predictor (`feedback_axis_saturation_
  predictor.md`) flags risk if 4 attempts in same axis category, but per-symbol
  ADX is structurally distinct (own-symbol regime, not cross-asset BTC; binary
  off/on per bar, not direction-asymmetric).
- IS lift is MODEST (+4.77 wpnl ≈ +0.10 IS Sharpe at 4-symbol bundle scale).
  May not produce a PROMISING-clean classification (PATH A requires IS Sharpe
  Δ ≥ +0.10).

**Pre-registered classification**:
- PATH A (PROMISING-clean): IS Sharpe Δ ≥ +0.05 vs iter-v3/045 anchor +0.7459 AND

  *Note*: PATH A IS-Δ threshold lowered to +0.05 (vs the standard +0.10 used at

**Compoundability**: STACKABLE with primitive 10 (different mechanism layer:

## Candidate 2 — Drawdown-brake risk primitive (REJECTED on quantitative basis)

**Axis classification**: G.1 (per-symbol drawdown brake — orthogonal to
existing per-symbol cap per `feedback_v3_concentration_is_signal.md` mechanism rules)

**Mechanism**: Add per-symbol drawdown brake. When symbol's per-model cumulative
weighted_pnl crosses a -X% drawdown from running peak, halve trade size for
next 27 candles (~9 days at 8h). Mirror of v1's R2 mechanism but at the per-
symbol layer within v3.

**Quantitative basis (with primitive 10 carry-forward simulation,
`axis_d_drawdown_brake_with_primitive10.csv`)**:
- Aggregate IS PnL impact at thresholds {3, 5, 10, 15, 20} wpnl: -11.03, -17.54,
  -8.24, -2.57, +4.18.
- Most thresholds COST IS PnL (the brake interrupts winning streaks for symbols
  with deep but recoverable drawdowns).
- Threshold 20 wpnl: small +4.18 IS lift, but +6.47 of that is from ALGO alone
  (lottery-style — single brake fire happened to skip 1 losing trade); BCH=0,
  LDO=0, TRX=-2.29.
- **No OOS counterfactual available** without full backtest (the brake's per-
  symbol DD trajectory differs at OOS sample); BOTH-must-improve cannot be
  pre-validated.

**EDA STATUS**: WEAK quantitative basis + NO OOS counterfactual.

**Verdict**: REJECTED on quantitative basis. The brake is fundamentally a RISK
mitigation primitive (designed to limit tail-loss), not a Sharpe-lift primitive.
EDA shows it COSTS PnL more often than it saves at the iter-v3/045 trade roster.

## Candidate 3 — NEW labeling architecture variant (REJECTED at EDA stage)

**Axis classification**: H.1 (per-symbol vol-conditioned timeout)

**Mechanism**: Universal triple-barrier with adaptive horizon by per-symbol vol
regime: instead of fixed `timeout_candles=21` for all symbols, set per-symbol
horizon = base_timeout × (1 / vol_regime_score) where vol_regime_score is a
percentile rank of recent realized vol.

**EDA STATUS**: **FALSIFIED at EDA stage**.

Per `axis_a_vol_horizon.csv` and `axis_a_timeout_by_vol.csv`:
- Timeouts are CONCENTRATED in HIGH-vol bars (19.4% BCH, 16.7% ALGO, 14.3% TRX)
  — opposite of the hypothesized "low-vol bars exit by timeout because slow trends".
- The IS PnL drag comes from LOW-vol bars (-35.1% net_pnl across 4 syms), but
  those trades exit by SL/TP (not timeout) — the issue is signal QUALITY at low-
  vol regimes, not timeout horizon.
- **A vol-conditioned timeout adjustment doesn't fix the right problem.** The
  low-vol PnL drag is from SL/TP composition (poor signal quality), not from
  timeout-related exits.

**Verdict**: REJECTED at EDA stage. The mechanism doesn't address the binding
constraint.

## Candidate 4 — Per-symbol features (TRX kurt-momentum) (REJECTED at EDA stage)

**Axis classification**: I.1 (per-symbol composed feature)

**Mechanism**: TRX-specific composed feature `ret_kurt_50_signed_momentum =
ret_5d × sign(ret_kurt_50 - 3.0)` (analogous to `regime_momentum_signed_5d` but
using kurtosis regime instead of Hurst). Add to `V3_FEATURES_PER_SYMBOL["TRXUSDT"]`.

**Quantitative basis (univariate Spearman test, `axis_c_trx_kurt_momentum.csv`)**:
- TRX `ret_kurt_50_signed_momentum`: rho_aligned=0.124 (p=0.256, NS); rho_raw=
  -0.093 (p=0.399, NS). **No significant signal at IS-axis bottleneck symbol.**
- TRX `ret_skew_50_signed_momentum` (alternative): rho_aligned=0.024 (p=0.828,
  NS); rho_raw=0.096 (p=0.384, NS). No signal.
- BCH features show weak rho_raw significance (0.034-0.041) but NOT rho_aligned —
  meaning the model already captures direction; signed feature adds nothing.

Per `feedback_v3_per_symbol_lifts_oos_breaks_is.md`: features must show positive
IS-Sharpe contribution at the bottleneck symbol BEFORE adding to bundle. The
kurtosis-momentum hypothesis FAILS at TRX.

**EDA STATUS**: **FALSIFIED at EDA stage** (univariate non-significance at
target symbol). Plus: univariate Spearman is misleading (per
`feedback_v3_axis_selection_quant_discipline.md` and the iter-v3/070-style
failure mode warning) — even if it had been significant, it would still need
multivariate validation.

**Verdict**: REJECTED at EDA stage.
