## Why other candidates were ELIMINATED

### (a) NEW labeling architecture variant — vol-conditioned timeout
**FALSIFIED at EDA stage.** EDA showed timeouts are CONCENTRATED in HIGH-vol bars
(19.4% BCH, 16.7% ALGO, 14.3% TRX) — **opposite** of the hypothesized "low-vol bars
exit by timeout because slow trends". The IS PnL drag comes from LOW-vol bars
(-35.1% net_pnl across 4 syms), but those trades exit by SL/TP (not timeout) — i.e.,
the issue is signal QUALITY at low-vol regimes, not timeout horizon. A vol-conditioned
timeout adjustment doesn't fix the right problem.

(See `axis_a_vol_horizon.csv` and `axis_a_timeout_by_vol.csv`.)

### (b) NEW model architecture — CatBoost head-to-head with LightGBM
**FALSIFIED on saturation grounds (informally) + cycle 3 budget.**
- Cannot be evaluated by EDA on iter-v3/045 trade roster — requires running a full
  model architecture swap.
- Implementation cost: ~3-5h to add CatBoost dependency + adapter strategy class +
  Optuna search space + adversarial tests. EXPLORATION wall-clock cap is 2h.
- Would consume the LAST EXPLORATION slot before CONFIRMATION on a high-risk model

- No EDA-derivable evidence that CatBoost would outperform LightGBM at the

  `feedback_v3_axis_selection_quant_discipline.md`.

If we were to take this on, it would warrant its own dedicated EXPLORATION cycle
(not the LAST slot before CONFIRMATION). Defer to iter-v3/051+ post-CONFIRMATION.

Plus: this is **univariate Spearman** which iter-v3/070-style failure mode warns
against (univariate-significant doesn't mean adds signal beyond existing 14-feature

## Candidate (e) detail — per-symbol ADX threshold

### Mechanism

Currently `RiskV2Config.adx_threshold = 20.0` is GLOBAL. iter-v3/049 axis: add
`adx_threshold_per_symbol: dict[str, float] = {}` field; for symbols in this dict,
use the per-symbol value; for others, fall back to the global threshold.

This is a NEW gate-config field (parallel to `regime_gate_symbols`,
`block_long_for`, `block_short_for`) that lets the universal ADX gate become
per-symbol asymmetric.

### EDA evidence (WITH primitive 10 carry-forward)

`axis_e_finegrained_adx_with_primitive10.csv` — per-symbol BOTH-must-improve
recommendation:

| Symbol | Recommended ADX threshold | IS lift (wpnl) | OOS cost (wpnl) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| BCHUSDT | **20** (no change) | +0.00 | +0.00 |
| LDOUSDT | **20** (no change) | +0.00 | +0.00 |
| TRXUSDT | **21** (raise +1) | +4.77 | -0.01 |
| ALGOUSDT | **20** (no change) | +0.00 | +0.00 |

Per-symbol ADX 21 sweep at TRX:
- 9 IS trades blocked at TRX ADX 20-21 (range 20.0 - 21.0)
- 2 OOS trades blocked at same ADX range
- 9 blocked IS trades had collective wpnl -4.77 (every single one was a net-EV
  loser)
- 2 blocked OOS trades had collective wpnl -0.01 (negligible — basically zero
  OOS impact)

### Why TRX specifically?

TRX has the FLAT importance distribution (top:bottom ratio 2.5×) and the
IS-axis bottleneck (-5.82 IS sum_wpnl). The model is searching for signal but
not finding it in the 14 features. EDA evidence:

- TRX trades at ADX 20-21 (just above existing global threshold) include 9 IS
  trades and 2 OOS trades.
- All 9 IS trades have weighted_pnl < 0 (collective -4.77 wpnl).
- 2 OOS trades have weighted_pnl ≈ 0 (collective -0.01).
- These are weak-trend trades (ADX < 21) where the model's already-weak signal
  is even less reliable.

By raising the TRX ADX threshold from 20 to 21, we filter out the weakest-trend
TRX signals (small but consistent IS lift) without sacrificing OOS edge.

### Why NOT BCH+TRX (Strategy A)?

Strategy A (BCH=21, TRX=21) WITHOUT primitive 10 simulation looked strong:
+20.22 IS / -0.17 OOS NET. BUT with primitive 10 ON, BCH ADX 21 produces:
- BCH IS lift +6.43 wpnl (only 5 trades blocked, smaller effect because BCH
  LONG was already blocked by primitive 10 — what remained was BCH SHORT which
  is HEALTHY)
- BCH OOS cost +4.42 wpnl (1 trade with +4.42 wpnl)
- NET +2.00 — but BOTH-must-improve FAILS (OOS cost > 0)

Per `feedback_v3_strict_both_is_oos_baseline.md`, OOS regression BANS the change.

### Why NOT BCH-only (Strategy C)?

Same reason as BCH+TRX: BCH ADX 21 fails BOTH-must-improve under primitive 10
(+6.43 IS / +4.42 OOS = mixed signal at single-seed; multi-seed CONFIRMATION
likely flips OOS negative).

### Compoundability

Per-symbol ADX threshold STACKS with primitive 10 (different mechanism layer:
ADX is regime-conditional kill switch; primitive 10 is direction-asymmetric
kill switch). Compatible with iter-v3/050 CONFIRMATION bundle.

If iter-v3/049 PROMISING-clean: TRX ADX 21 carries forward to iter-v3/050
CONFIRMATION as a CANDIDATE bundle ingredient. Multi-seed validation required
(per `feedback_v3_engineered_features_dont_stack.md` deferral; this is NOT a
new feature but a NEW gate, so the rule is loosely analogous).
