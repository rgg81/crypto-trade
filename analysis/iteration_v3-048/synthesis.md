choice. Per `feedback_v3_strict_both_is_oos_baseline.md`, the gate would FAIL the
BOTH-must-improve rule at multi-seed CONFIRMATION. Axis A is FALSIFIED at the EDA

## Recommended axis: Candidate 2 — `vol_normalized_ret_5d`

**Why vol_normalized_ret_5d over the other 4 candidates**:

1. **Built from rank-1 feature for TRX** (`range_realized_vol_50` mean rank 3.00 across
   4 symbols, but rank 1 for TRX where the IS-axis bottleneck lives). The composed
   feature exposes TRX-specific structure that the model currently can't represent at
   depth-3-5 LightGBM splits. **TRX has FLAT importance distribution (4.0× ratio top:

2. **Canonical Sharpe-like ratio** — vol-normalized return is the textbook risk-adjusted
   momentum (Sinclair *Volatility Trading*; LdP AFML Ch. 8). Conceptually defensible vs the
   ad-hoc sign-flip compositions.
3. **Distinct from `regime_momentum_signed_5d`** in primitive (uses range_realized_vol_50,
   not hurst_100). Per `feedback_v3_engineered_feature_pivot.md`, the IC carve-out
   applies for Category 2 composed features; importance ≥30 is the binding gate.

   (range_realized_vol_50 already shifts).
