## STRUCTURE DIAGNOSIS — CRV is case (b): pure-noise / no learnable structure

Confirmed on three independent axes. **This is the central finding of the iteration.**

1. **Win rate ~30% in BOTH directions on a symmetric label = anti-signal, not no-signal.** Long WR 31.2% (96 trades), short WR 28.2% (85 trades). On a near-symmetric triple-barrier label a directionally-blind model floors at ~33% three-class / ~50% binary; **30% in both signs means the model is systematically picking the wrong side** — it has fit IS noise into a sign-pattern that inverts out-of-sample (and the OOS WR 30.3% confirms the inversion is stable, not a fluke). A noise symbol with no exploitable structure produces exactly this: the depth-5 head finds a spurious IS sign-rule, commits to it with conviction (mean confidence 0.403 spanning the full 0–1 range), and that rule is anti-correlated with realized forward returns. ## REFINEMENT RECOMMENDATION — add a SECOND selector gate: learnable-structure pre-screen

The negative-baseline selector needs a structure gate placed BEFORE any symbol enters a specialist brief. Concrete, IS-only, three-tier — implement as a hard pre-flight filter:

**GATE 2 — Learnable-Structure Pre-Screen (IS-only, run in `analysis/iteration_v1-NNN/structure_screen.py`):**

1. **PRIMARY (decisive, cheap): fast single-seed LightGBM probe IS Sharpe.** Train ONE LightGBM (`max_depth=5`, `num_leaves=31`, single seed=42, `n_trials=10`, the full 48-feature stack, the exact triple-barrier label) walk-forward on the candidate's IS window. Read the probe IS Sharpe.
   - **Filter: probe IS Sharpe ≥ +0.30 → PASS to brief. < +0.30 → REJECT, do not author a specialist.**
   - Rationale: this is the lowest-cost direct measurement of "can a depth-5 head extract anything here." A no-structure symbol (CRV) returns a near-zero or negative probe; a structure-present symbol (DOT/AAVE) clears +0.30 easily. CRV would have been rejected at the probe stage — at single-seed n_trials=10 it would have surfaced the anti-signal WITHOUT spending a full 50-seed × 30-trial specialist run. **One probe (~minutes) replaces one catastrophic specialist (~hours).**
2. **SECONDARY confirmer (catches probe basin-lottery): max single-feature |IC| on IS.** Compute Spearman IC of each of the 48 features vs the forward triple-barrier label sign, IS-only, walk-forward-averaged. Take the max |IC| across features.
   - **Filter: max single-feature |IC| ≥ 0.04 → structure present. < 0.04 → noise.** (Calibrate the exact threshold on the DOT/063 + AAVE/078 winners vs CRV/084 + FIL/083 losers — those four are the labeled training set for this gate. CRV's best within-family IC magnitudes in `ic_matrix.csv` are family-redundancy, not feature-vs-label; the screen must compute feature-vs-label directly, which this iteration did NOT.)
3. **TERTIARY (informational, do not gate alone): return-autocorrelation magnitude.** CRV showed |autocorr| < 0.026 at all lags — near-zero persistence. Report max(|acf_lag1|, |acf_lag3|, |acf_lag7|); flag if < 0.03 as a no-persistence WARNING that reinforces a borderline probe/IC reject.

**Combined selector rule going forward:** a symbol enters a specialist brief iff `trivial_baseline_min_horizon ≤ +0.15` (GATE 1, negative-baseline, unchanged) **AND** `probe_IS_Sharpe ≥ +0.30` (GATE 2 primary) **AND** `max_feature_|IC| ≥ 0.04` (GATE 2 secondary). Negative-baseline AND structure-present = true headroom (case a). Negative-baseline AND structure-absent = the CRV trap (case b) — rejected before it can crater. The probe Sharpe is the load-bearing gate; IC and autocorr are confirmers that prevent a single-seed probe from being fooled by basin-lottery.
