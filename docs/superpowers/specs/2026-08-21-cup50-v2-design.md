# CUP-50 v2 — Generalization-First Binance Top-50 Tournament, Edition II

Design spec + implementation plan (designed by Fable 2026-08-21, approved by the owner; executed by Opus). Worktree: `quant-portfolio-blind-top50-v2`
(branch of the same name, forked from `quant-portfolio-blind-top50` @ `b584c011`).

## 0. Context — what CUP-50 was, and what it was not

CUP-50 (2026-08-17) built the best tournament *infrastructure* in the lineage: immutable namespace, weekly
Top-50 point-in-time universe by 180-day median quote volume, pure-crypto fail-closed classification,
checksum-bound two-pass acquisition, physically separated IS/sealed snapshots, network-disabled read-only
Docker evaluation, hash-chained journals, one-shot observation, atomic release, exact-replay paper desk.
`src/crypto_trade/cup50/` (19 modules, ~7k LOC, 73 tests) is self-contained and reusable.

But the *research* phase never happened:

- Every team's "strategy" is a 27–46-line organizer seed; every nomination is `official-centre-001`;
  **12 of 144 official trials were used**. No team iterated. The leaderboard measures regime luck of naive seeds.
- **4 of 12 lanes were cost-annihilated** (turnover 231–937×/yr, 3×-cost drawdowns 68–91%).
- **The 3×-cost cell at 50% weight made IS non-discriminating**: no lane was profitable at 3× in IS; 8 of 12 IS
  scores were < 0.05. The OOS "winner" (team-12, S 33.8) ranked **9th in IS with score 0.00014**.
- The **official winner was changed after the sealed read**: a "top-3 IS qualifiers" stage was applied
  retroactively (incident 3, self-described as "cannot recreate pre-OOS finalist blindness"); team-02
  (breakout, S 31.2) replaced team-12 and now runs the paper desk.
- The common risk unit (90-day *realized* strategy vol) did not control risk: the winner ran at **23% vol
  against a 10% target** because its book was flat 50% of the time then 20%/name.
- Three organizer incidents in one day: readiness strategy was flat (exercised nothing), placeholder bars
  treated as listings, post-exit coverage stopped at roster exit, funding assumed ≤1 settlement/8h.
- Team-visible IS data: bars (HLCV, quote volume, trade_count, taker-buy volumes; `open` censored),
  funding (rate, interval); no marks, no OI. Context window = trailing **180 days** only.
- Sealed OOS regimes (measured): F1 Feb–Aug 24 BTC +53% / median alt −17% (bull + alt bleed); F2 Aug 24–Feb 25
  BTC +59% / alts +5% (broad bull); F3 Feb–Aug 25 BTC +13% / alts −38% (dispersion, alt bear); F4 Aug 25–Feb 26
  BTC −32% / alts −59% (full bear); F5 Feb–Aug 26 BTC −20% / alts −31% (bear). IS = 2021 bull, 2022 bear, 2023 chop.

Cross-edition lessons that survived (from TOP-40 v1 → CUP-20): the cost wall, not signal absence, is the
binding constraint; IS rank carries ~no information about OOS rank at the top (CUP-20 top-3 rank corr = −1);
score the plateau not the peak (neighbourhood); no scoring/qualification change after the first sealed read;
"no winner" must remain a legal outcome; the tournament never authorises capital, only the forward record does.

**Goal of v2 (user):** find a strategy/model that generalizes across bull, bear and chop; winner(s) go to a
6-month paper stage before real money. Same 12 teams. Teams must not see prior strategies. All anti-leak /
anti-cheat rules remain. Organizer (Fable) seeds every lane with a creative starting strategy.

**User decisions (2026-08-21):** (1) keep the CUP-50 IS/sealed split and reuse the audited acquisition, with
organizer-contamination disclosed; (2) all 12 lanes observed; winner = highest OOS S among teams clearing a
pre-registered IS robustness **bar**; (3) final stage = top-3 + an equal-risk ensemble on **4 parallel paper
desks**, real-money pick by a pre-registered rule; (4) staffing = **1 Opus research agent per lane + 1
read-only Opus Critic**.

## 1. What stays immutable (reused verbatim from CUP-50)

- Universe: weekly (Mon 00:00 UTC) first-50 USDT-M perps by 180-complete-day median quote volume, no
  hysteresis, lexical tie-break, pure-crypto fail-closed, archive-derived listing episodes, no substitution,
  causal unavailability force-settlement, post-exit execution coverage.
- Data scope: 8h canonical bars + funding (+ hourly marks organizer-side). **No OI / L-S ratio / spot added**
  (explicit non-goal: every prior data expansion produced organizer defects; boundary-pushing goes into
  mechanisms, not plumbing).
- Windows: replay from `2021-03-15`; IS `[2021-03-15, 2024-02-01)`; sealed `[2024-02-01, 2026-08-01)`;
  five 6-month OOS folds. Acquisition root copied bit-for-bit from
  `../quant-portfolio-blind-top50/data/cup50/acquisition-remediated-20260817` (checksum-verified raw bytes —
  explicitly permitted reuse).
- Interface: `DecisionContextV2` (bars w/o `open`, funding w/o marks, trailing window — widened to 365 days
  by C21, eligible symbols),
  `build_strategy()` → `target_weights(context, *, seed)` → weights / `{}` flat / `None` hold.
- Execution: fill at transaction open of `t`; 5 bps fee + 2.5 bps slippage per side; cost multipliers
  1×/2×/3× run independently; gross ≤ 1.0, |net| ≤ 1.0, symbol ≤ 0.20, participation ≤ 0.001 of bar quote
  volume; funding to `(t, t+8h]`, every settlement valued at its hourly mark; equity ≤ 0 ⇒ candidate failure.
- Team volatility targeting forbidden; common risk unit mandatory.
- Integrity: clean-room scan of team roots; Docker network-none / read-only / cap-drop / non-root evaluation;
  quarantine receipts; hash-chained journals; one-shot freezes; silent observation; atomic release;
  missing / leaking / non-causal / mutated / crashed entry = permanent DNF, never replaced.
- Paper: exact replay of the tournament evaluator, append-invariant public-data cache generations,
  watchdog/healthcheck/digest, any strategy change = new lineage.

## 3. Scoring (complete, frozen)

Per point / window / cost multiplier, on daily-compounded net returns (`_daily_frame` unchanged):

```
g = 365/n Σ log(1+r_net)      D = max drawdown (net)     v = std(gross daily)·√365
a = fraction of days with gross exposure ≥ 0.05           u = min(1, v/0.10, a/0.50)
h = top-5 |gross day| share (1.0 if flat)
x = g − 0.50·D − 0.10·(1−u) − 0.05·max(0, (h−0.25)/0.75)
q = 50·(1 + tanh(x/0.10)) clamped to [0,100];   q = 0 if a < 0.05            (C6)
C = 0.45·q1 + 0.35·q2 + 0.20·q3                                                 (C4)
G = Σ weights·sorted_ascending(fold C)  — OOS weights (.40,.25,.20,.10,.05); IS weights (.35,.25,.15,.12,.08,.05)
R = .50·min + .30·mid + .20·max over regime C  (BULL/BEAR/CHOP; <60 days ⇒ dropped, renormalise)   (C5)
A = C on the whole window
P = 0.60·G + 0.25·R + 0.15·A                                                    (C5)
S = 0.50·median(P_k) + 0.25·P_low + 0.25·P_centre,   P_low = sorted position ceil(K/4)
```
Ranking: eligible before ineligible before DNF; then higher S, P_low, min point, centre, centre worst-fold 3×;
lower centre 3× drawdown; lower turnover; bundle SHA-256; team id. Decimal round-half-even 1e-6.
Regime labels are computed once at `build` from the organizer snapshot and written into both manifests; the
rule is public, the sealed labels are not.

## 5. Research field — 12 lanes and organizer seeds

Lane list (revised: `calendar-settlement-seasonality`, `relative-value-convergence`, `liquidity-shock-reversal`
and `volume-confirmed-trend` were cost-annihilated or redundant; replaced by event-reversal, cluster-reversal,
attention-flow, a learned model and breadth timing). Every team receives its mandate, the seed below as
`strategy.py` + `parameters.json`, the toolkit, the playbook — and nothing else.

**Seed conventions (all lanes, implemented once in the organizer-owned `cup50v2/toolkit.py`, exported in the
protocol bundle, read-only; teams may copy/modify inside their own file):**
- Daily decision at 00:00 UTC bars (others return `None` = hold) unless stated; **EMA-smoothed targets**
  `w_t = (1−λ)·w_{t−1} + λ·raw_t` kept as strategy state (phase-agnostic, turnover-smoothing); **no-trade band**:
  return `None` if `Σ|w_t − w_last_emitted| < band`.
- **Vol-parity** per side: weight ∝ signal/σ_30d, each side normalised so long = short = 0.5 gross for neutral
  books (gross 1.0); symbol weight hard-capped at 0.15 inside the seed.
- `σ_30d` = std of 8h log returns over 90 bars × √1095. Returns, ranks and z-scores are cross-sectional over
  `eligible_symbols` at `t`. Only `close`, `high`, `low`, `quote_volume`, `trade_count`,
  `taker_buy_quote_volume`, funding are used.
- **365-day context (C21)**: anything needing longer memory (the learner's expanding panel) accumulates its
  own state from the stream and stays flat until warm (allowed by charter: state reconstructed from past-only
  context). Seeds must return `None`/`{}` gracefully with < 5 eligible symbols or short histories (the bundle
  test feeds a single-symbol context).
- `seed` argument is honoured wherever randomness exists (L09 clustering init, L11 none needed).
- Every seed ships with: `parameters.json` centre + 2–4 declared dimensions, a pre-registered **regime
  expectation table** (bull/bear/chop/dispersion: +/−/0), a **turnover budget**, and three **frontier
  directions**. Expected seed turnover 25–80×/yr.

## 10. Limitations disclosed in the charter (not to be "fixed" silently later)

1. Sealed-window organizer contamination (user-accepted; forward desks are the uncontaminated test).
2. LLM-agent hindsight about 2021–2026 markets cannot be policed beyond date-literal scans, preregistration,
   neighbourhood robustness, regime stratification and the forward record.
3. 2.5 years of OOS cannot finely rank survivors (SE ≈ ±0.7 Sharpe); hence 4 desks, not 1.
4. Research-eval approximates execution (last close, funding at close); official trials are authoritative.
5. Organizer-side blindness during scoring remains detection (transcript audit, canary) not OS-level prevention.
6. The 365-day context window still forces the learner lane to warm up inside the replay (first ~6 months flat),
   which its IS activity term prices.
